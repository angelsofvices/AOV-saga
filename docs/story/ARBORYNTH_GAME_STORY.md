## 1. Story Overview

RP8 takes place on **Arborynth**, Planet 4 of the Aethryx Expanse.

Arborynth is **The Living World**: an interconnected planetary ecosystem whose forests, plants, creatures, environments, and deeper biological systems function as parts of a greater living whole.

A mysterious **cosmic pollution** has begun entering and spreading through this living ecosystem. Contaminated natural creatures can become rabid, violent, unstable, hostile, and destructive. Their corruption threatens settlements and damages the ecological systems they once helped maintain.

The player character becomes involved in this crisis and ultimately must:

> **Save Arborynth from the cosmic pollution destroying its living ecosystem.**

The story begins at a smaller, local scale. The player may initially encounter aggressive wildlife, creature attacks, damaged habitats, threatened settlements, and strange environmental changes. Over time, these events are revealed as symptoms of a planetary ecological infection.

### Thematic Core

RP8 revolves around:

- Life
- Balance
- Corruption
- Adaptation
- Restoration

The player is not simply fighting monsters. Many hostile creatures are natural Arborynth organisms corrupted by an outside influence. Where appropriate, story and gameplay should distinguish between:

- **Defeating a creature**
- **Saving or restoring a creature**

The larger objective is restoration of Arborynth's ecosystem, not indiscriminate destruction of everything affected by the crisis.

Arborynth is not merely a planet covered in nature. Its ecosystem is an interconnected living system. Forests, plants, creatures, natural environments, and deeper biological systems participate in a greater planetary whole.

## 3. Ancient History

Arborynth's original life consisted of native natural and verdant organisms originating from its ecosystem and from Orivora.

Humanoids are **not native to Arborynth**. Their arrival occurred later through the Nexyrosillian Transplacement.

Additional ancient history is **TBD**.

## 4. Orivora / The Great Root

**Orivora** is the World God of Arborynth and is known as:

> **The Great Root**

Orivora is a towering natural deity and the living embodiment of Arborynth's forest and natural ecosystem.

### Forms

Orivora possesses:

- A male form
- A female form

These are not separate gods. Both are natural manifestations of the same divine being.

### Self-Procreation

Orivora is capable of **self-procreation**. Through this ability, Orivora creates and populates Arborynth with natural verdant lifeforms that help maintain the planetary ecosystem and fulfill different ecological purposes.

Orivora's creations may include:

- Plants
- Creatures
- Verdant organisms
- Intelligent natural species
- Large ecological organisms
- Other lifeforms necessary for environmental balance

The exact taxonomy and species roster remain open for later development.

### Role in Arborynth

Orivora does not merely rule Arborynth. Orivora actively contributes to the creation and continuation of its living ecosystem.

The deeper relationship between Orivora and Arborynth itself remains an important mythological area for future development.

## 5. Nexyrosillian Transplacement

The **Nexyrosillian Transplacement**, caused by **Egnellahc**, displaced humanoid populations across the Aethryx Expanse.

Some displaced humanoids were transplaced onto Arborynth. These populations eventually established settlements and civilizations and became Arborynth's humanoid population.

Arborynth therefore has two broad historical population origins:

### Native Verdant Life

Life originating from Arborynth's natural ecosystem and Orivora.

### Transplaced Humanoids

Humanoid populations whose ancestors arrived following the Nexyrosillian Transplacement.

By the era of RP8, Arborynth is home to both. Modern humanoids must not be portrayed simply as temporary outsiders. Their ancestors were displaced there historically, and Arborynth has become their home.

## 6. Modern Arborynth

Modern Arborynth is inhabited by native verdant life and descendant populations of transplaced humanoids.

## 8. Power System

The primary form of power on Arborynth is the use of **natural objects as conduits of power**.

Arborynth's cultures learned to use naturally occurring materials and objects to interact with the planet's natural energies.

Many categories of natural power objects may eventually exist. Except for Aurastaffs, no additional category is currently canonically defined.

## 9. Aurastaffs

**Aurastaffs** are the most common and culturally significant power conduits on Arborynth.

They are usually created from branches of **ancient Arborynth trees**. An Aurastaff allows its wielder to channel or interact with natural power.

Aurastaffs are a primary visual and gameplay identity of RP8. They are not generic fantasy wizard staffs.

## 10. Cosmic Pollution

A mysterious cosmic pollution has begun entering and spreading through Arborynth's natural ecosystem.

The pollution negatively affects living organisms. Natural creatures exposed to sufficient contamination can become:

- Rabid
- Violent
- Unstable
- Hostile toward other organisms
- Dangerous to humanoid settlements
- Destructive toward their own ecosystems

Creatures that normally participate in Arborynth's ecological balance become monsters that actively damage it.

### Cascading Threat

1. Cosmic pollution enters the ecosystem.
2. Natural creatures become corrupted.
3. Corrupted creatures attack ecosystems and citizens.
4. Ecosystems begin collapsing.
5. The living world becomes increasingly damaged.
6. Arborynth itself may eventually die.

The pollution is more than a monster outbreak. It is a **planetary ecological infection**.

Its origin, cause, intent, and relationship to Orivora are not yet known.
