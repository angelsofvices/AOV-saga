# AOV™ SAGA · TYPE EFFECTIVENESS CHART — PROPOSAL v1
**Status: AWAITING CREATOR APPROVAL — not wired into RP7 (§39).**

## Design principles

1. **Every edge is a sentence from the Saga.** No bracket exists without a canon reason you could speak aloud in a Codex entry.
2. **Structure stays disciplined:** 20 types, every type deals super-effective damage to exactly **3** types and takes it from exactly **3** — no dead types, no kings, no mutual pairs (if A beats B, B never beats A).
3. **The great axes shape the graph:** the **Light class (Astrums)** and **Mass class (Dracolords)** oppose through their champions (Astra vs Draconic); the **Divine/Corrupted war** runs one direction at a time; the **Beast→Creature lineage** flows downhill; **Ultharis-substrate cosmology** puts Ultimate outside the graph entirely.
4. **Multipliers (proposed):** strong ×2 · reverse ×0.5 · duals multiply (4× / 0.25× reachable) — same math the shipped chart uses. **ULTIMATE: ×3 vs everything, no weaknesses, Ultimate-vs-Ultimate = ×1** (stats decide the duel of gods) — unchanged from the shipped rule.

## Reading the weaknesses (automatic, no separate table)

Each type's 3 weaknesses are simply who beats it — e.g. **Aquatic** falls to Draconic, Creature, Humanoid; **Draconic** falls to Divine, Astra, Ultramax; **Corrupted** falls to Aquatic, Radiant, Divine.

## Notable stories the graph tells

- **Draconic is the most contested crown**: beaten only by Divine, Astra, and Ultramax — heaven, the opposing class, and the manufactured rival.
- **The Divine/Corrupted/Radiant triangle** plays the holy war without mutuals: Divine and Radiant both burn Corrupted; Corrupted answers by striking Spirit, Aura, and the Gemshard miracle instead.
- **Aquatic lands as a real type**: quenches Elemental (your "living flame" ontology makes this literal), cleanses Corrupted, drowns Nature — and is answered by dragons, fishers, and sailors.
- **Chrono/Astra/Crystal** form the cosmology corner: crystal holds time, astral bends time, crystal binds astral.
- **Ultimate stays off the board**, as the substrate should — Ultharis is not IN the taxonomy; the taxonomy explains Ultharis.
