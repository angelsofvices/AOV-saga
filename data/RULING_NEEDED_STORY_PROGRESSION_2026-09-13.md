## ★★★★★ R2 · WHEN DOES RIZER GET S2? *(highest cost)*

**New progression** — at the emergency return to Malezor, five districts before
the finale:

> "**RUBY RESONANCE · RAGE · S2 UNLOCKED.** … Rakoron senses what is happening
> inside him."

**Your ruling, 2026-09-01** (`ZORYN_ARC_CANON.md`, `EGNELLAHC_SPLIT_PROPOSAL.md`):

> *"**he cant have s2 until endgame battle against xenoxil.** he uses his s2 form
> to fight zoryns s2 form in part 2."*

### What moving it early costs, concretely

- **The Rakoron deferral.** Canon has Rakoron refuse Rizer *twice* and arrive at
  the finale to say *now* — "the god who twice said *not yet* arrives at Korathen
  and says *now*." The new version has him merely **sense** it mid-game, and the
  arrival scene disappears.
- **Xenoxil's Tier VIII.** He was promoted to T8 precisely so that "a Tier VIII
  arrives, and grants the power that lets a mortal fight a Tier VIII." Grant S2
  five districts early and the finale's power budget loses its justification.
- **★★★ The Zoryn climax.** Part 2's boss fight is **S2 versus S2**, and its
  entire engine is that Rizer was *refused and accepted the refusal* while Zoryn
  *took* it: "the fight cannot be decided by power, because the power is
  identical. It is decided by the only thing that differs — **how each of them
  answered a no**." Rage-triggered S2 *is* taking it. That is Zoryn's road.

### ★ There is a version that keeps both

The new doc's emotional beat — parents taken, Rage, **Rakoron senses it** — is
genuinely better staging than what canon had. It only conflicts if *sensing* and
*granting* are the same moment.

> **Malezor: Rakoron senses the Ruby resonance and does not grant it.**
> **Korathen: he arrives and grants it.**

That keeps your 2026-09-01 ruling, keeps the deferral, keeps the T8 arithmetic,
keeps S2-vs-S2 — and *gains* the new scene, because a refusal at the moment of
his parents' abduction is far crueller and better than the ones we had.
**Needs your yes.**

## ★★★★★ R4 · WHERE IS THE PART ONE FINALE?

**New progression:** Korathen is Act IV (Ophira II + Orryx II, Xenoxil escapes);
the finale is Act V at the **Bridge of Hope**, Xenoxil + both Chiefs together.

**Shipped canon:** Korathen holds "the **three-Seer battle**" as *one* fight, and
the Bridge is explicitly **not a battlefield**:

> "**THE BRIDGE OF HOPE** — south of Baelgor/Xilnar. Opens only when both paths
> are complete. A **ceremonial corridor** into the Part 2 southern lands, and the
> site of a **farewell: Mom · Dad · Kelthor · Myara.**"

### Three consequences

1. **★★★ The gate becomes circular.** The shipped unlock is
   `xenoxilDefeated ✔ + novarianGrandChampion ✔` → *then* the Bridge opens.
   Fighting Xenoxil **on** the Bridge requires the Bridge to open before he is
   defeated. One of the two has to move.
2. **`novarianGrandChampion` vanishes.** The Bridge currently also gates on Path A,
   the Novarian Challenge. The new progression doesn't mention it.
3. **★★ The ending changes.** Canon's Part 1 climax is *"The Throne Remains
   Empty"* — Rizer refuses to replace one absolute ruler with another and
   restores authority to the districts. The new ending is "Upper Zyraxis saved"
   plus the Lower Zyraxis reveal. **The Empty Throne refusal is not in the new
   progression at all.** It is described as Part 1's thematic climax in three
   separate documents.

★ **Also:** splitting the single three-Seer battle in two means Ophira and Orryx
are now fought **three times each**. Twice is a rematch; three times is a
pattern, and the second one has no new information to deliver.

**Needs your call: does the finale move, or does the Bridge stay ceremonial?**

## ★★★★ R3 · IS LUMINARY FORM THE SAME THING AS S3?

**New progression:** they are separate. **Pearl → S3 → Balance** (condition not
yet established), and **Luminary Form** = natural Lv100 **+** Anciuxor bond,
outside the Gem ladder.

**Shipped canon and shipped art:** they are one thing.

> "**S3 RIZER · LUMINARY FORM.** Unlocked at **Rizer Level 100** — Tier 8, the
> maximum a mortal reaches."

`idle-luminary.png` has been live in-game since **v0.96.40**, and the asset pack
commissions **22 sheets** all named `*-luminary.png` **as the S3 set**.

### ★ Two things that make this easier than it looks

- **Pearl is already in the S3 palette.** The Luminary art direction is "white,
  **pearl** and warm gold." *Pearl → S3* is supported by the shipped art, not
  contradicted by it. **Only the split from Luminary breaks.**
- **The Anciuxor requirement is new but reads right.** He is already the hardest
  bond in the game (bond 100, tier 10, Lv100), so "elite achievement" is exactly
  what bonding him already is.

**So the question is narrow:** is Luminary the *name* of S3 (keep them one,
adopt Pearl/Balance as its gem, optionally add the Anciuxor bond as a second
requirement), or is there a **fourth** form? If it's a fourth, 22 commissioned
sheets need renaming and S3 needs its own art.

## ★★★★ R1 · WHERE DOES S1 COME FROM?

**New progression:** Malezor, from Rizer's own affinity for intellect, no Gemlord.

**Shipped canon**, under a heading reading *"★ The S1 → S2 order is **locked**"*:

> "**S1 · Azurel (Vorashil).** Perception, clarity, controlled Aura circulation.
> **Not fuelled by anger.** Azurel opens and stabilises the channel."

Three moves in one: the **source** (Gemlord → innate), the **district**
(VI → I), and the **meaning** (perception/clarity → intellect).

★ **Sapphire itself is not in dispute** — Azurel is the Sapphirelord and the S1
weapon is the Sapphire Tearsword. Only the giver and the place moved.

★★ **The cost:** Vorashil already lost its mission-7 turn when the kidnapping
relocated to Malezor. It was left holding "S1 + the first Orryx defeat, which is
enough for a district." **Take S1 out and Vorashil has only Orryx I**, and Azurel
becomes a Gemlord with no story function.

   **Anciara / the Immortal Council**. A "Divine Council" and an "Immortal
