# RP7 CANON LOCK · The Ninth and Tenth Gem Formations

**Status:** Approved canon · supersedes all prior ten-natural-type language
**Ruled:** 2026-09-12 by the Creator
**Spoiler class:** ★★★ DEEP COSMOLOGY — see §9 before using ANY of this publicly

---

## THE RULING, IN ONE LINE

> **The first eight gems are discoveries of nature.
> The Ninth and Tenth are discoveries of Egnellahc.**

Zyraxis has **eight fundamental Gem Types**. The Ninth and Tenth are not
additional natural types — they are **Composite Gem Formations**, produced by
the higher-order convergence of the eight.

---

## 1 · The Mothergem reconciliation

The Mothergem remains the source of Zyraxis's gem architecture and of the power
expressed through all ten Gemlords. It contains **eight fundamental principles,
not ten independent natural types**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **RETIRED** | The Mothergem shattered into ten equivalent fragments, and one Gemlord rose directly from each. |
| **CANON** | The Mothergem revealed eight fundamental principles, from which the first eight Gemlords arose. Egnellahc later converged those principles into the Ninth and Tenth Composite Formations. His division brought forth Oathane and Oatheus, completing the Ten. |

- The **Ninth Formation** combines the principles of the **First Four** Gem Types.
- The **Tenth Formation** combines the principles of the **Final Four**.

Both were formed *within Egnellahc*. The final two Gemlords therefore derive from
Mothergem architecture, but **not** as two ordinary Mothergem fragments.

## 2 · The forbidden convergence

Egnellahc was the first — possibly the only — being to discover that the eight
types were not isolated categories but **two halves of one deeper structure**. He
created and held both Composites at once, bringing the complete architecture into
a single existence.

It did not hold. Space, matter, perception and the boundaries between dimensions
began to fracture around him.

His real achievement was not stronger gems. It was the proof that the eight
belong to a unified structure — **and that reuniting that structure is
catastrophically unsafe.**

## 3 · The division

To stop the fracture, Egnellahc irreversibly divided his own essence, redistributing
what had become too dangerous to hold together.

| Gemlord | Formation | Converges | Keeps its association with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oathane** · Ninth | Ninth Composite | the First Four | balance, system, anomaly, ordered structure |
| **Oatheus** · Tenth | Tenth Composite | the Final Four | divinity, law, totality, finality |

Neither is a descendant, a creation, or a naturally born Gemlord. They are
**separated continuations of Egnellahc's essence** — the living consequences of
the convergence.

## 4 · Aftermath

- Egnellahc was **raptured into the Third Dimension** and brought before **Ultharis**.
- Oatheus inherited knowledge of the **World and Space Gems**, and is compelled to
  conceal their existence and nature from mortal life — which is what prevents
  mortals from deliberately recreating the convergence.

## 5 · Locked cosmic law

> **Reality cannot safely contain the complete unification of what the Ninth and
> Tenth Composite Formations represent.**

Governs any future story touching: reunifying Oathane and Oatheus · reconstructing
Egnellahc's undivided power · World or Space Gem research · convergence of all
eight fundamental types · dimensional instability from gem architecture · mortal
attempts to manufacture a new Ninth or Tenth.

## 6 · Continuity preserved

Zyraxis still has ten Gemlords. Oathane and Oatheus are still the two results of
the Egnellahc split, still hold their ninth/tenth associations, and still belong
to the greater Mothergem cosmology. **No gameplay statistics, districts, roles or
roster placements change.**

## 7 · Naming

**Oathane** is canonical. **Oathare** is obsolete legacy terminology.

---

## 8 · ★★★ SPOILER BOUNDARY — read before writing anything public

### MAY be stated publicly
- There are **eight fundamental Gem Types**.
- Two exceptional **Composite Gem Formations** exist beyond the eight.
- Zyraxis nevertheless has **ten Gemlords**.
- The Ninth and Tenth **differ in origin** from the first eight.
- Their true origin is **not publicly understood**.

### MUST NOT be stated publicly
Egnellahc created the formations · IX = First Four, X = Final Four · that he held
both at once · that the convergence fractured reality · that he divided his
essence · that Oathane and Oatheus emerged from that division · his rapture into
the Third Dimension · his audience with Ultharis · the existence or function of
the **World and Space Gems** · Oatheus's duty to conceal them.

### Approved spoiler-free wording

> For most of recorded history, eight Gem Types were believed to define the
> natural limits of gem formation. Zyraxis also recognizes two exceptional
> composite formations whose structure and origin remain closely guarded. From
> these ten expressions come the Ten Gemlords — but the final two do not share
> the same origin as the first eight.

### ★ ANSWERED · 2026-09-12

> **Creator:** *"1. Oathane is the name. 2. a gems color just alludes to its
> astralite makeup and the color theory of the saga."*

**1 · Naming.** `Oathane` confirmed; `Oathare` stays obsolete. The only surviving
occurrence in the repo is inside a dated backup snapshot — history, left alone.

**2 · What a colour IS.** This dissolves the apparent conflict rather than
choosing a side. Colour was never the gem's taxonomic identity — it is an
**allusion**, pointing at two things at once: the gem's **astralite makeup**, and
the saga's own **colour theory**.

So `PRISMSHARD_GEMSHARD_CANON.md`'s *"a gem's TYPE is its COLOUR"* is not
overturned, it is explained: colour is how you read what is inside. That is also
why it never contradicted the COLOUR ⊥ FAMILY rule in the colour-theory doc.

★★ **And it closes the count.** `GEM_COLOR_PSYCHOLOGY_CANON.md` already records,
from an earlier ruling: *"Aethryx is the light, the 63 Astralites are what
happens when it leans, and a white gem is a trace of it that never did"* — and it
counts **eight gem colours**. Eight colours, eight fundamental Gem Types. The two
documents were agreeing before either of us noticed.

★ **One inference, offered not asserted:** if colour alludes to astralite makeup,
the Ninth and Tenth — convergences *of* the eight rather than any single lean —
would have **no colour of their own**. That reads as a feature. The macrobook now
uses it as its one permitted hint: *"The last two have no colour of their own."*
Say the word if they should carry hues and I will revise.
