## 1. COSMOLOGY

### 1.1 · The Astralite Matrix (Master Codex v12.2)
- **63 canonical Astralites** organized as **9 Families × 7 tiers**:
  Tier 1 = Core (1 per family) · Tier 2-4 = Major (3 per family) · Tier 5-6 = Minor (2 per family) · Tier 7 = Mythic (1 per family)
- **AETHRYX** = universal source (the substrate every Astralite draws from)
- Every item / relic / weapon / inhabitant DNA carries an `astraliteSignature` `{primary, secondary, trace, aethryx: true}`
- **27 planets** classified as Genesis / Omen / Definitive
- **Zyraxis = Ax-9 Astryx Soul**
- **5 + 9 Ascension formula** = the Seer endgame target (why they seek Xenoxil)

### 1.2 · Astralite Signature System
Every Zyrex (and every ally, item, terrain feature) DNA carries a signature. Nine Mortal Branches → nine DNA-family starter maps. Five element-interaction crafting formulas govern combining signatures. This is the game's physiochemistry, not decoration.

### 1.3 · The 16 Astralite Prisms (V3.17.50–52)
All 16 Prisms named + planet-placed:
- **Zyraxis-native obtainable (3)** — Prism of the Deep (Xilnar) · Prism of Refuge (Vorashil) · **Prism of the Crown** (Korathen · Grand Champion mantle)
- **Zyraxis-native unobtainable via Rizer Path** — Prism of Omnithris = **the Key of Anciuxor** (auto-satisfied by bonding Anciuxor per V3.17.52 · Ultimate Prismsynch remains the endgame ceiling and is separate from the Grand Champion prize)
- **Off-world (12)** — distributed across Origon (2) · Quorana · Lumeria · Pyrauna · Arborynth · Cytherion · Elythera · Ignara · Uralyx · Viridia
- **Wanderer's Prism** = mobile, POV device
- **★ Key of Anciuxor** = attached to Anciuxor's tail — bonding Anciuxor means possessing the Key
- Each of the 16 Prisms keys ONE unique Prismsynch

### 1.4 · Prismsynch (the endgame move)
`Prismsynch` is the ultimate **move type** (not an object) that fires when THREE locks align:
1. Max Rizer Level
2. Max Zyrex Bond
3. Correct Astralite Prism in hand

Each of 16 Prisms keys ONE unique Prismsynch. The **Key of Anciuxor Prismsynch = ULTIMATE** = communion with Ultharis. Because the Key is on Anciuxor's tail, bonding Anciuxor auto-satisfies lock 3, making the Ultimate Prismsynch reachable inside the RPG.

### 1.5 · Prismshards (crafted vessels)
Small crafted bonding vessels. **Prehistoric Zyraxian ritualists** carved them from district gemstone substrate (Ruby / Pearl / Onyx / Amethyst / etc.). **DIRECT PROGENITOR OF THE MODERN ZYSPHERE** — Thardun engineers refined the substrate + shape into the mass-produced Zysphere. Bond Masters can still channel through surviving ancient Prismshards for techniques mass Zyspheres cannot replicate. Kelthor teaches this at Step 8 (Rizer Lv 50).

### 1.5b · The Three Utility Prisms of Zyraxis (2026-07-17 lock)
Geometric trinity — every practical Zyraxian tech device is one of three shapes. Distinct from the 16 cosmic Astralite Prisms. Thardun mass-manufactured.

- **ZYSPHERE (sphere · bonding)** — catches / bonds Zyrex. Click-to-release, hold-to-bond. Variants: Basic · Void · Ultra. Refined descendant of the prehistoric Prismshard.
- **ZYCUBE (cube · storage)** — holds items / materials. Six identical Z-emblem square faces. The universal Zyraxian inventory vessel. Canonically the player's inventory IS a Zycube; PC Storage architecture underlies excess Zyrex spheres.
- **ZYRAMID (pyramid · power)** — energizes / powers other prisms. Two operation modes:
  - Base-attachment: the square base plugs into any square face (Zycube face, another Zyramid's base, any Thardun-standard interface) to supply resonance
  - Sphere-insertion: a Zysphere inserted inside the Zyramid amplifies its bonding potential

The **Zyramid's asymmetry** is what makes it the POWER unit — its square base is the interface side. Sphere + cube are terminals; the Zyramid is the source. Together they cover BOND · STORE · POWER for daily Zyraxian tech.

Cross-medium: appears identically in cards / mobile / console / books / movies / collectibles. Same trinity everywhere.

### 1.6 · Aenoric Convergence
Every 7-11 years, the Expanse aligns planets into a "+" crucifix of light — an EVENT, not the default state. Default depiction remains natural distributed orbits.

### 1.7 · Ovauron / World 28
= Primalutonia-28, "The Worldender," the drift world. Violet, rogue inclined orbit, consumes worlds, approaching Earth. World 28 is separate from the ordered 27.

## 2. HISTORICAL LINEAGE

### 2.1 · Cytherion (Planet 8) — origin of insectoid life
- Formed ~9.2 Bya
- Homeworld of ALL Insectoid life (Branch IV of the 9-branch mortal taxonomy)
- **First artificial structure in the cosmos = "the Grid"** (built here)
- Egg-site of **Mykarlyth** — the FIRST humanoid individual — hatched here before migrating to Myraclese
- Synthara (Future/Systems Astralite · Family 8 core) anchors Cytherion. Cognara (Mind/Thought · Family 4 core) anchors Arborynth. Insectoids are an **astralite-origin race** — a race can anchor to an astralite saturation, not only a homeworld

### 2.2 · The Synthrax (evolved insectoid clade)
- Umbrella name for ALL futuristic insectoid-humanoid beings of Cytherion
- Spans true-insectoid + arachnid lineages
- Externally formidable · internally a JUST, PEACEABLY-GOVERNED civilization

**Locked castes:**
- **Mantis** — apex intelligence · rulers + strategists (HARD LOCK — most intelligent)
- **Spider** — trappers · ambushers · assassins (HARD LOCK — war aggressor)
- Ant / Hornet-Wasp-Bee / Beetle / Roach / Dragonfly ◆ proposed

### 2.3 · The Cytherion Bug War (Founding War · HARD LOCK)
- SPIDERS attempt to seize control of insectoid-kind — conquest by stealth
- MANTIS form a SMART ALLIANCE uniting weaker castes; lead the DEFENSE
- Mass casualties; peace bought in blood
- **OUTCOME**: Mantis fought to PROTECT rather than RULE → earn a peaceful legitimate reign by EARNED CONSENT
- **SPIDER FATE**: subdued + reintegrated under Mantis order, kept to the margins — reconciled yet watched · active-threat status DORMANT

### 2.4 · Cytherion → Zyraxis migration
**Option C locked**: mixed refugee wave (Mantis civilians + Spider dissenters) fled the Bug War, 200M-yr transit, blended over eons into the Alienlands Synthrax-Astryx clade.

### 2.5 · Mykarlyth → Egnellahc → Oathane / Oatheus
Three-planet humanoid arc:
- **Cytherion** (Mykarlyth's birth)
- **Myraclese** (Mykarlyth's dominant reign — ruled by force, imposed hierarchy)
- **Zyraxis** (Mykarlyth's SON **Egnellahc**'s Accord — REJECTING his father's pattern)

Egnellahc = the first humanoid on Zyraxis. He later split into **Oathane** (Ninth Gemlord, Thardin) + **Oatheus** (Tenth Gemlord, Korathen). Every Zyraxian humanoid is spiritually descended from Egnellahc's rejection of his father Mykarlyth's dominant-ruler pattern.

- Tamed **RAKORON** through trust, not force
- Sanctioned by **OATHANE** (post-split) — because Oathane sees himself in Novarius: both are first-humanoids on their respective era, both refuse Mykarlyth's pattern

### 4.1 · Types (20-type chart)

- Beast ← Draconic
- Aura ← Radiant / Divine / Aquatic
- Nature ← Verdant / Elemental
- Spirit ← Chrono / Astral
- Tech ← Crystal
- UV ← Corrupted

### 4.2 · T × 333 stat pool rule
Every species base stat pool = **Tier × 333**. Auto-enforced in build_codex.py + boot audit in the game.

### 4.3 · Growth curves (V3.17.53 · Pokemon-style tier-based)
- T1 = Fast (800K to L100)
- T2-3 = MedFast (1M)
- T4-5 = MedSlow (1.06M)
- T6-7 = Slow (1.25M)
- T8+ = Fluctuating (1.64M)

Replaces the old linear formula that only summed to ~42K by L100.

### 4.4 · XP reward formula (V3.17.54)
```
xp = floor(defeatedLv × (8 + tier×8) × battleTypeMult × levelDiffMult × XP_GLOBAL_MULT)
```
- Battle-type multipliers: wild 1.0 · trainer 1.5 · commander 2.5 · gemlord 4.0 · endgame 2.5
- Level-diff clamp: `clamp(0.6 + 0.4 × ratio, 0.5, 1.8)` — rewards fighting up

### 4.5 · The 5 canonical statuses (V3.17.39)
- **Overcook** (ATK-decay)
- **Psychache** (DEF-decay)
- **Undershock** (SPD-decay)
- **Gridfreeze** (SPC-decay)
- **Souldrift** (HP-decay)

Uniform pct-decay. One per opponent stat.

### 4.6 · Move-unlock schedule + 4-slot canon
Every Zyrex learns exactly 4 moves on a fixed Lv 1 / 15 / 30 / 60 ladder. Slots (V3.17.58):
- **A1** (top-left) = primary damage
- **A2** (top-right) = secondary damage
- **S1** (bottom-left) = support — tertiary/blend damage + status inflict (30%)
- **U1** (bottom-right) = ultimate — bigger damage + status inflict (45%), species-name prefix ("GNASHLING'S BEASTFALL")

Runtime auto-generated via `buildCanonMoves` for all 158 species. `MOVE_STATUS_BY_TYPE` maps each type to its canonical status.

### 5.3 · Revive rule (V3.17.35)
Potions do NOTHING on KO'd Zyrex. Only:
- **Revival Stem** (from Mom's plant / tree shake) → FULL HP + clears status

### 5.4 · Item acquisition (V2.68)
Items ONLY come from chests / NPC dialogue / world finds. NEVER auto-granted on walk-in.

### 5.5 · No easter-egg spoilers

### 5.6 · Exploration loop (CORE GAMEPLAY)

### 5.7 · Sidequest philosophy
