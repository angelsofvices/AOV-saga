# 0. CANON GOVERNANCE

## 0.1 Prime Rule
Everything explicitly established by the Creator as AOV™ Saga lore is binding canon unless the Creator later explicitly revises, supersedes, or corrects it.

Future development must:
- preserve established facts;
- expand, connect, clarify, or deepen canon;
- distinguish confirmed canon from hypotheses and unresolved material;
- never invent a retcon merely to solve a continuity problem;
- reinterpret obsolete public wording conservatively when newer Creator canon clarifies the intended meaning.

## 0.2 Highest Authority
The **Highest One** is the singular ultimate God/Creator and the most consistent metaphysical anchor of the entire Saga.

Everything else exists downstream of the Highest One:
- creation and existence;
- Anciuxor;
- Aenor and Zoryth;
- the Astralite Matrix;
- Mass and Light;
- Astrums and Dracolords;
- Planetelles;
- worlds and realms;
- Immortals;
- Gemlords;
- humanoids;
- the Cosmic Mirror;
- the Eternal War.

No created being is a competing supreme deity.

**Canon axiom:**  
> Creation can be mirrored. The Creator is not.

The Cosmic Mirror can produce many religious, mythological, philosophical, symbolic, or cultural echoes, but it does **not** create multiple Highest Ones.

## 0.3 Canon Priority
When sources conflict, use this priority:

1. Latest explicit Creator statement.
2. Explicit Creator correction/lock from canon-development conversations.
3. Current approved master canon documents.
4. Current official website/codex/game material that does not conflict with 1–3.
5. Older published wording, interpreted in the least-retconning way possible.
6. Assistant theories, pitches, connective ideas, and hypotheses — **not canon unless adopted by the Creator**.

- Aethryx Expanse = fuller/high-activation expression
