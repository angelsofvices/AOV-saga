## 1 · ★ THE V3.5 STORY SUPER-LOCK

This is the big one. The pre-V3.5 Seer story (Ael'Tharion + generic gemshard theft for personal Rizer power) is entirely superseded.

**New motivation (locked 2026-07-13):**

The Seers are not stealing Gemshards for personal Rizer strength. That is only Phase 1 of a much larger ritual. Their true objective is **the conquest of Zyraxis**.

Throughout the game the Seers are secretly kidnapping both Zyrex and humanoid civilians from every district. Villagers assume wild attacks, accidents, people running away, isolated crimes. Only late in the game does the player realize every disappearance is connected.

The victims are being **sacrificed to Xenoxil**. Xenoxil uses this life-energy to strengthen himself while preparing Orryx and Ophira for ascension to pseudo-immortality.

**Full plan:**
1. Recover all ten Gemshards.
2. Use accumulated sacrifices to elevate Orryx and Ophira toward pseudo-immortality.
3. Defeat every Gemlord.
4. Seize each Fathergem.
5. Rule Zyraxis permanently.

The Gemlords are the FINAL obstacle to Xenoxil's conquest — not the enemy the story is about.

**Codex updates needed:**
- **Retire the name AEL'THARION as founder of the Seers.** Every codex row / dialog / lore reference should now say **XENOXIL**. Xenoxil is Tier VII Pseudoimmortal, the founder.
- Add a Codex canon page or update the existing Seer page with the sacrifice-ritual plot.
- Orryx (Tier V Apex) and Ophira (Tier V Apex) unchanged as roles, but each gets a fresh characterization pass — see §2.

**Memory anchor:** `rizing-powers-story.md` (V3.5 SUPER-LOCK).

## 2 · ✿ THE THREE SEER LEADERS — updated characterizations

### XENOXIL — the Fleshlord (Tier VII Pseudoimmortal, founder)

- **Origin:** the Fleshlord of another world, off-Zyraxis. He arrived carrying that title.
- **Philosophy:** does not value humanoid life the way native Zyraxis civilizations do. Views life as biological fuel.
- **Signature line (canon):** *"If a thousand lives forge one eternal guardian, then those thousand lives served their purpose."*
- **Presentation:** calm, logical, emotionally detached. Rarely raises his voice. His greatest weapon is his complete lack of compassion.
- **Codex flavor to insert into his row:** scenes are quiet, monochrome, unhurried. No music swell. Terrifying at a whisper.

### ORRYX — the Reluctant Believer (Tier V Apex)

- **Not purely evil.** Genuinely believes eternal leadership is the only path to preventing future wars.
- Sees Xenoxil's promise of immortality as a **necessary sacrifice** (his own words, internal).
- Wants order. His tragedy: he slowly compromises his morality in pursuit of stability.
- Should get doubt beats in future dialog / codex worldsense. He almost defects at least once.

### OPHIRA — the Prophetess (Tier V Apex)

- Treats Xenoxil almost like a prophet.
- Believes the sacrifices are sacred.
- Her followers **willingly participate.** They believe they are creating a perfect future.
- **Aesthetic:** quiet religious atmosphere — choirs, hymns, echoes, unsettling serenity. Beauty as horror.

**Memory anchor:** `rizing-powers-story.md` §"three Seer leaders — updated characterization".

## 3 · ★ THE GEMLORD PACT (new canon lore lock)

Long ago the Ten Gemlords made a **sacred agreement**: they would remain within their sanctuaries and never directly intervene in mortal affairs unless absolutely necessary. Their self-imposed isolation protects both their Fathergems and the balance of Zyraxis.

**This is why the Gemlords do not defeat the Seers themselves.** Sanctuaries stay sealed through the entire main story. Only after all 10 Gemshards are restored do the sanctuaries open — and even then, Gemlord duels are **optional** post-endgame content, not story fights.

**Codex updates needed:**
- Add a **new codex page** or entry for the Gemlord Pact.
- Every Gemlord's row should carry a brief "bound by the Pact — cannot intervene mid-story" note.
- The Gemlord respect-fight arc (V2.22 canon: post-endgame Lv 100 friendly duels) still stands — that's the Pact loosening after the shards return.

**Memory anchor:** `rizing-powers-story.md` §"The Gemlord Pact".

## 4 · ✿ FOUR NEW RECURRING CHARACTERS

Each is significant enough to want a codex row or short entry.

### 4.1 THE QUIET CHILD
- Never introduced. Never explains themselves.
- Appears near shrines and sacred locations across every district.
- Carries the same worn stuffed Zyrex toy — the through-line visual cue.
- Appears in impossible places without explanation.
- **Late-game reveal:** the child's parents were taken by the Seers. The child mistook the player for a Seer ally (they saw the player entering Ministry buildings, mistaking infiltration for allegiance).
- **Endgame beat:** challenges the player to a battle. After losing, finally speaks. After Xenoxil falls, forgives the player.
- **Theme:** hope surviving grief. The stuffed toy is the concrete symbol.
- **Memory anchor:** `aov-quiet-child.md`

### 4.2 DAD'S FORMER MENTOR
- One of the oldest living scholars on Zyraxis.
- **Personally knew EGNELLAHC** before Egnellahc split his essence into what became **Oathane (IX) and Oatheus (X)** — this is a critical lore anchor that ties directly to the codex's existing Egnellahc-split note.
- Speaks in fragments. Signature tone: *"People speak of the Ninth and the Tenth. I remember when there was only One."*
- **Primary lore-drop source** for: Xenoxil's Fleshlord origin, the Egnellahc split, the Verdant Awakening as felt on Zyraxis, the Gemlord Pact origin.
- **Post-story:** optional SUPERBOSS. Not hostile — he wants to test whether the next generation is worthy of inheriting Zyraxis. Team of Tier VII/VIII rarities at Lv 100.
- Reward: high-prestige title, suggested "Heir of the Ancient Word."
- **Memory anchor:** `aov-dads-mentor.md`

### 4.3 THE WANDERING MERCHANT
- Comedic. Constantly drinking.
- Always telling ridiculous stories — most contradict each other; some are true.
- Somehow survives impossible situations.
- Banned from several towns (each ban a specific running gag).
- Still welcomed everywhere because he brings news, rare items, and a laugh.
- Sells escalating inventory as the player's Rizer Level rises (see §7).
- Never explains where he goes between towns. Ambiguous whether he's more than he seems.
- **Sample canon-nod lines already in game (V3.10.1):**
  - "I saw the Rubylord blink. Everyone says he never blinks."
  - "Ophira sent me a hymn once. I sang it backwards on a dare."
  - "They say the Fleshlord invented the sales tax."
  - "I visited Netharion once. I don't remember it."
- **Memory anchor:** `aov-recurring-characters.md` §"WANDERING MERCHANT"

### 4.4 THE JOURNALIST — player's childhood best friend
- Runs an **intergalactic radio broadcast** known throughout the Aethryx Expanse.
- Travels alongside the player's journey (parallel path, not a party member).
- Interviews villagers, Rizers, scientists, travelers, merchants, survivors in every district.
- Player helps repair transmission equipment and complete broadcasts throughout the game.
- **Payoff:** by the endgame, listeners from other worlds tune in. The player's actions become known across the wider Aethryx Expanse.
- Connects Zyraxis's story to the broader saga cosmology without dragging in off-world characters directly.
- **Memory anchor:** `aov-recurring-characters.md` §"THE JOURNALIST"

## 7 · ✧ RIZER LEVEL SYSTEM (V3.8)

New player-side progression, **separate from Zyrex level**.

- Grows through: chests opened, NPC / grunt / Commander defeats, sidequest completions.
- **XP curve:** `rizerXpForNext(lv) = 100 + lv*50 + floor(lv² * 0.6)`.
- **No hard ceiling.** Scales cleanly past Lv 100 for post-endgame Gemlord respect duels.
- HUD shows: `★ R.Lv N · XP/NEED`.
- Unlocks better merchant stock past thresholds (see §8).

**Codex should note:** this is player-side. Every codex row that references "level" for a Zyrex is UNRELATED to Rizer Level.

## 15 · ★ SIDEQUEST DESIGN PHILOSOPHY (canon rule for all future quests)

**Every sidequest must answer at least ONE of these five questions:**

1. Who lives here?
2. What happened here?
3. What is changing here?
4. Who is lying?
5. What does the player become?

The main story is deliberately focused and straightforward. The depth of Zyraxis emerges through its optional content. Sidequests are not filler — they are how the world becomes worth saving.

Codex should either accept this as a design principle page or reference it in the game/TCG design section.

**Memory anchor:** `aov-sidequest-philosophy.md`
