# Rejected bag icons

Art that was generated and keyed but is **not wired**, kept here so it is not
regenerated by accident and not silently re-imported by a future pass.

## `zysphere-drop.png` — rejected 2026-09-23

Creator: *"do not use the new zysphere icon."*

It came back as a Poké Ball: dark upper hemisphere, light lower hemisphere,
equatorial band, circular lens centred on the band. The generation prompt had
asked for the opposite in as many words — *"NOT a Poke Ball: the seam glows and
the halves are offset, asymmetric"* — and the render did not honour it.

`assets/zysphere.png` (unwired, 1254², fully opaque, photoreal 3D) is the same
silhouette with a chrome finish and a Z on the lens, so it is not a substitute.

Until replacement art exists, `zysphere` has **no entry in `ZYCUBE_ART`** and
the slot falls back to its 🔮 glyph — which is exactly what the emoji fallback
layer is for.

**A replacement needs to break the silhouette, not restyle it.** The recognisable
part is the shape: two hemispheres split by a band with a round centre button.
Any one of these breaks it — a housing that is not a sphere; a seam that is
off-axis or spirals rather than ringing the equator; a lens that is offset,
multiple, or absent; an open frame rather than a closed shell.
