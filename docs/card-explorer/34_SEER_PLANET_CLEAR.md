# Survey 10.35 · Seer Outposts and Planet Clear

Each ordinary planet now carries three to five Seer commanders. A Seer outpost consists of:

- one stronger Seer commander;
- a nearby three-member Aethren team;
- a nearby three-to-five-member Mori or Daemon auxiliary group.

The existing live combat system handles every group on the overworld. Seers are stronger than ordinary patrols, and all enemy health, attack, and defence values have been raised to keep the expedition challenging as Carl gains more weapons and Aethren.

The current visit keeps defeated groups gone. Leaving the planet and returning rebuilds its patrols. The Seer progress for the planet persists: once every Seer is defeated, the refugee camp recognizes the planet as cleared and offers rescue, subject to the existing Habitation Zone and language requirements.

Cave rooms also carry denser, stronger enemy placements. No card-battle screen is introduced; Aethren fight automatically in the live overworld system.
