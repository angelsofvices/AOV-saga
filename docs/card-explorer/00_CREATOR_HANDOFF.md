## Project Concept

Create a new web-based, mobile-first AOV™ Saga game combining:

- Exploration
- Card collecting
- Player progression
- Deck building
- PvE encounters
- Online card battles
- AOV™ lore discovery
- Physical AOV™ card integration
- Cross-game integration with RP7

The game serves as an interactive introduction to The AOV™ Saga. A player who knows nothing about the universe should be able to enter as the protagonist and discover the Aethryx Expanse naturally.

The game's story may eventually become an official AOV™ Saga prequel/spinoff.

## STORY PREMISE

### Earth — 1936

The player is an astronaut participating in a highly classified United States mission.

Humanity has secretly developed an experimental spacecraft capable of beginning exploration of the Solar System far earlier than publicly known history suggests.

During the mission, the spacecraft experiences a catastrophic malfunction.

Navigation fails.
Earth communications disappear.
The vessel is unexpectedly thrust into hyperspace and thrown completely off course.

When the spacecraft finally exits hyperspace, none of its star charts correspond to its surroundings.

The astronaut has unknowingly entered:

**THE AETHRYX EXPANSE**

The astronaut initially has no knowledge of the Expanse, its terminology, civilizations, Astralites, planets, history, or connection to Earth.

The player's original objective becomes simple:

**Explore. Survive. Document what has been discovered. Find a way home.**

## CORE PLAYER FANTASY

The player becomes the first known Earth explorer systematically documenting the Aethryx Expanse.

The fundamental gameplay loop is:

**TRAVEL → LAND → EXPLORE → DISCOVER → COLLECT → ARCHIVE → BUILD → BATTLE → PROGRESS**

The player gradually visits the worlds of the Aethryx Expanse and discovers its creatures, people, locations, materials, artifacts, technology, history and mysteries.

Everything collectible is represented by a digital card.

## THE 1936 EXPERIENCE

1936 is not simply the date of the story.

The entire human side of the game should feel like 1936.

The astronaut begins with period-appropriate or secret experimental Earth technology rather than modern technology.

Visual and interface influences include:

- Analog gauges
- Mechanical counters
- Radio equipment
- Vacuum tubes
- Physical switches
- Dials
- Levers
- Riveted metal
- Paper star charts
- Typewriters
- Government dossiers
- Field journals
- Scientific catalogues
- Film photography
- Military/scientific expedition equipment

Avoid presenting the astronaut as a modern person wearing vintage clothing.

Modern terminology should also be avoided when the astronaut would not know it.

Early terminology should favor:

Field Record, Specimen, Observation, Photographic Record, Expedition Log, Star Chart, Radio Transmission, Instrument Reading, Classification, Survey, Dossier, Catalogue.

The astronaut initially attempts to understand the Expanse through 1936 Earth science.

Aethryx terminology is learned through exploration.

For example, an Astralite may initially appear in the player's records as:

**UNKNOWN CRYSTALLINE SPECIMEN**

After the astronaut learns the correct terminology, the record can be reclassified as:

**ASTRALITE**

The interface therefore evolves alongside the player's knowledge.

## SPACECRAFT

The astronaut's original spacecraft functions as the primary home base.

It contains:

- Navigation
- Expedition records
- Card inventory
- Mission logs
- Equipment
- Ship upgrades
- Travel controls
- Communications
- Research/classification tools

Initially the spacecraft is entirely based on experimental 1936 Earth engineering.

As the player explores, they begin incorporating Aethryx technology and Astralites.

The ship gradually becomes a unique hybrid:

**1936 Earth engineering × Aethryx technology**

The original analog/riveted construction should remain visually identifiable underneath the increasingly advanced modifications.

This creates an Aethryx-dieselpunk visual identity for the protagonist.

## PLANET EXPLORATION

The player can visit the worlds of the Aethryx Expanse one by one.

Each world functions as:

1. An explorable game destination.
2. A major chapter of the expedition.
3. A collectible card set.

Planetary exploration should involve actual explorable environments rather than exclusively menu-driven expeditions.

Potential activities include:

- Exploring wilderness
- Visiting settlements
- Entering caves
- Discovering landmarks
- Meeting inhabitants
- Encountering creatures
- Photographing discoveries
- Recovering artifacts
- Finding Astralites
- Completing missions
- Discovering hidden locations
- Surviving environmental hazards
- Unlocking historical records
- Fighting PvE battles

Players do not necessarily need 100% completion to continue.

Completionists can return to previously visited worlds to finish their collections.

## AETHRYX BIOLOGICAL CLASSIFICATION

Two major established classifications must be preserved.

### AETHREN

Creatures within the Aethryx Expanse are classified as: **Aethren**

### HAEMEN

Humans/humanoids are classified as: **Haemen**

Both classifications should be further specified by their world and, where applicable, their district of origin.

Example:

```
CLASS: Aethren
WORLD: Zyraxis
DISTRICT: Malezor
SPECIES: [Specific Species]
RARITY: 7/10
```

A Haemen follows the same general system.

Planet and district specificity is important to AOV canon.

## RARITY SYSTEM

Card rarity uses a: **1–10 SCALE**

However, rarity does NOT mean conventional card scarcity.

The card's rarity number maps directly to the subject's established AOV canon tier.

Therefore:

- Canon Tier 1 = Rarity 1/10
- Canon Tier 7 = Rarity 7/10
- Canon Tier 10 = Rarity 10/10

Canon is the authority.

Encounter frequency, acquisition difficulty and card scarcity are separate mechanics.

A Tier 3 / Rarity 3 subject could therefore be considerably harder to discover than a Tier 6 / Rarity 6 subject.

Do not alter canonical tier merely for card-game balancing.

## CARD COLLECTION PHILOSOPHY

Anything meaningfully collectible within the game can manifest as a card.

Potential card subjects include:

- Aethren
- Haemen
- Characters
- Astralites
- Artifacts
- Armor
- Weapons
- Plants
- Technology
- Vehicles
- Locations
- Structures
- Historical discoveries
- Cosmic phenomena
- Abilities
- Important events
- Other significant AOV discoveries

Cards belong to the appropriate celestial/world set.

The sets effectively become visual records of the astronaut's journey through the Expanse.

## DIGITAL CARDS ARE INVENTORY ITEMS

Digital cards are not merely an external card-gallery abstraction.

**CARDS MANIFEST AS ACTUAL INVENTORY ITEMS.**

When a player successfully collects something, its card enters the player's in-game inventory.

Example:

```
CARD: [Name]
SET: 09 — Zyraxis
CLASS: Aethren
WORLD: Zyraxis
DISTRICT: Malezor
SPECIES: [Species]
RARITY: 6/10
QUANTITY: 1
```

The player can manage collected cards from their inventory.

Inventory cards eventually support systems such as:

- Deck building
- Battles
- Trading
- Collection management
- Cross-game redemption
- Other card-specific uses

## INVENTORY VS. ARCHIVE

These should remain distinct systems.

### ARCHIVE

Records what the astronaut has discovered.

It represents knowledge, lore and completion.

Example: **ZYRAXIS ARCHIVE — 63% COMPLETE**

### INVENTORY

Represents the actual digital cards the player currently owns.

A player may therefore have discovered something for their Archive without necessarily possessing multiple copies of its usable card.

## DISCOVERY SYSTEM

Discovery is one of the game's primary mechanics.

The player should not simply receive everything through randomized card packs.

Cards can be acquired by actually interacting with the Aethryx Expanse.

Potential methods include:

- Finding
- Observing
- Photographing
- Studying
- Capturing evidence
- Completing quests
- Winning encounters
- Receiving gifts
- Trading
- Recovering objects
- Exploring hidden locations
- Completing planetary objectives

The card reveal becomes the reward moment.

**DISCOVERY → CLASSIFICATION → CARD MANIFESTATION → INVENTORY**

## CARD BATTLES

Collected cards can be assembled into decks.

The game will eventually support:

- PvE battles
- Casual online PvP
- Ranked online PvP
- Deck building
- Player progression

The exact battle rules, deck size, battlefield structure, resource system and turn structure remain to be designed.

The battle system should ultimately feel native to AOV lore, rather than simply copying an existing TCG.

## PHYSICAL CARD INTEGRATION

Real-world AOV™ physical cards connect directly to the digital ecosystem.

Players will eventually be able to:

**SCAN / REDEEM PHYSICAL AOV™ CARDS**

A successfully authenticated physical card unlocks its corresponding digital ownership/reward.

The digital card then manifests within the player's card inventory.

The final production system should use secure unique identifiers such as serialized QR codes, NFC, unique redemption codes or another backend-verifiable method to prevent unlimited duplicate redemption from photographs of the same card.

## RP7 INTEGRATION

Compatible digital cards can also connect to: **RP7**

The intended ecosystem is:

```
PHYSICAL AOV™ CARD
↓
SCAN / VERIFY
↓
DIGITAL CARD OWNERSHIP
↓
AOV CARD INVENTORY
↓
REDEEM COMPATIBLE CONTENT IN RP7
```

The exact RP7 reward depends on the card.

Examples may include:

- Zyrex
- Equipment
- Armor
- Astralites/resources
- Items
- Cosmetics
- Special collectibles
- Missions
- Other compatible RP7 rewards

Not every card must literally manifest its subject inside RP7. Rewards should respect RP7 gameplay and AOV canon.

## UNIVERSAL AOV ACCOUNT — LONG-TERM DIRECTION

The larger goal should be a shared AOV™ player ecosystem.

Rather than every game having completely isolated ownership:

**ONE AOV ACCOUNT → SHARED IDENTITY → SHARED CARD OWNERSHIP → MULTIPLE AOV EXPERIENCES**

This new card game can become the central collectible layer connecting:

**Physical AOV Cards ↔ Digital Card Game ↔ RP7 ↔ The Wider AOV™ Saga**

## PRIMARY DESIGN PILLARS

The game currently has five core pillars:

- **EXPLORE** — Travel through and investigate the Aethryx Expanse.
- **DISCOVER** — Encounter things no Earth human in 1936 understands.
- **COLLECT** — Turn discoveries into collectible digital inventory cards.
- **ARCHIVE** — Document worlds, districts, species, history and AOV lore.
- **BATTLE** — Build decks from collected cards and compete in PvE and online PvP.

The narrative motivation underneath everything remains:

**FIND A WAY HOME.**

The astronaut believes returning to Earth is the mission.

The larger experience gradually becomes about documenting and understanding The Aethryx Expanse.
