## I. THE FIXED CONTINUITY

The historical chain is linear:

> **Viridia → Andre Hart → Auraxion → Zyraxis → Rizer → Aurellyn → Carl Nasaro → Earth → Mutagenesis**

**Andre Hart and Auraxion are the same person.** Before coming to Zyraxis, Auraxion was **Andre Hart of Viridia**. This is pre-existing AOV canon and cannot be changed.

Auraxion later reaches Zyraxis and becomes an ally of **Rizer** during RP7. Auraxion assists Rizer's quest and gives him the **UFO**.

Auraxion's son is **Aurellyn**.

Aurellyn is later **trained by Rizer**, directly carrying the RP7 legacy forward into the later Saga chronology.

Auraxion was also a great **Astralnaut**, creating a legacy that profoundly influences his son. Aurellyn wants to become a great Astralnaut and explorer in his own right.

This lineage eventually leads directly to Earth.

# II. 1936 — CARL NASARO REACHES ZYRAXIS

**Carl Nasaro** is an ordinary Earth human of exceptional intelligence.

He is not initially superpowered, divinely chosen, secretly Aethryx-born or descended from an extraterrestrial lineage. His humanity is fundamental to his role.

Carl serves as the realism anchor between Earth and the Aethryx Expanse.

In **1936**, Carl participates in an extremely classified American experimental space mission intended to explore humanity's Solar System.

Something goes catastrophically wrong.

Carl's spacecraft is displaced far beyond its intended destination.

He reaches:

# **ZYRAXIS**

Carl has unknowingly become the first known Earth astronaut to enter the Aethryx Expanse.

His original mission is effectively over.

A new expedition begins.

# III. THE LIVING MASTER CODEX

Carl has two overriding objectives:

> **COLLECT THE EXPANSE.**
>
> **MAP THE EXPANSE.**
>
> **BRING IT HOME.**

He explores Aethryx from the perspective of a human whose scientific understanding ends in **1936**.

He documents Aethren, Haemen, Astralites, worlds, districts, civilizations, technologies, artifacts, locations, historical evidence, armor, weapons, phenomena and other discoveries.

Collectibles manifest through the game's card/inventory system.

His expedition archive becomes the basis of the **Living Master Codex**.

The game itself functions as the continuously expandable playable Master Codex of The AOV™ Saga.

As new chronologically compatible AOV canon is established, it can be incorporated into the game as new discoveries.

The governing rule is:

> **The AOV™ Saga establishes canon.**
>
> **The Living Master Codex allows players to discover it.**

The game does not overwrite established history.

# IV. CARL NASARO & AURELLYN

During Carl's years in Aethryx, he eventually meets **Aurellyn**.

Carl tells Aurellyn about Earth.

Earth fascinates Aurellyn precisely because it is **nothing like the Aethryx Expanse**.

For Carl:

**Earth is home.**

For Aurellyn:

**Earth is an undiscovered frontier.**

Aurellyn's desire to find it is deeply connected to Auraxion.

His father was a great Astralnaut.

Aurellyn wants to prove himself capable of making a discovery worthy of that legacy.

Carl and Aurellyn consequently share a destination for completely different reasons:

**Carl wants to rediscover Earth.**

**Aurellyn wants to discover Earth.**

But neither can locate it.

Carl's accidental journey into Aethryx does not provide usable coordinates for returning home.

Earth remains lost.

# V. 1945 — EARTH REVEALS ITSELF

Nine years after Carl disappeared, humanity enters the atomic age.

Earth's **1945 nuclear detonations produce a detectable ping through the Aetherstride**.

This is crucial canon.

Carl's stories do not give Aurellyn Earth's cosmic location.

**The 1945 nuclear Aetherstride ping does.**

For the first time, the distant world Carl has spent years describing can actually be located.

Carl can finally go home.

Aurellyn can finally make the great Astralnaut expedition he has dreamed about.

Together:

# **CARL NASARO + AURELLYN → EARTH**

This closes the main 1936–1945 expedition narrative.

# IX. UFOs, AREA 51 & THE CONSPIRACY CULTURE

The period from 1945–1955 becomes the hidden foundation of AOV Earth's famous extraterrestrial mythology.

But there is an important canonical rule:

**Not every conspiracy theory is secretly true.**

Instead, truth and fiction become deliberately difficult to separate.

Some sightings involve Aurellyn.

Some involve classified aircraft.

Some result from secret research.

Some witnesses actually encounter something extraordinary.

Some stories are misunderstandings.

Some are completely fabricated.

**Area 51 and related UFO mythology become part of this environment.**

The public sees rumors.

Carl sees what exists behind them.

This ten-year period is officially:

# **THE CONSPIRACY ERA — 1945–1955**
