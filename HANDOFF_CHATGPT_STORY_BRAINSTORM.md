## 1 · Who I am and what we're doing

I'm the creator of **The AOV Saga** (Angels of Vices LLC) — an original-IP cinematic universe across four realms: **Origon** (origin world), **Viridia** (bloodline world, saga anchor for books/films), **Zyraxis** (gem-forged realm, anchor for the TCG/game), and the **Aethryx Expanse** (the binding star system holding all 28 worlds).

The game we're brainstorming for is **Rizing Powers of Zyraxis** — a top-down Pokémon-like RPG built as a single HTML file. Core mechanics are locked (types, stats, battle math, catch formula, districts, evolution chains, gemshard economy). What's OPEN:

## 2 · The world in three paragraphs

**Zyraxis** is the ninth world of the Aethryx Expanse — a gem-forged realm divided into **10 canonical Districts (I–X)** in a Z-shape across the map, connected by named bridges (Bridge of Hope, Green Divide) and bordering waters (Northern Void Sea, Southeast Hydroplane). Each district is ruled by one **Gemlord** — a Tier VIII Immortal being tied to a specific gem. The Gemlords are dormant, sealed inside their sanctuaries by their own ancient **Gemlord Pact** (see §7). They are respected, not hunted.

The living beings are **Zyrex** (singular and plural — like "sheep"). Any non-Rizer creature native to any planet in the Expanse is a Zyrex. Trainers who bind them are called **Rizers**. The player is a Rizer. The Cardmaster TCG's 20-type system governs Zyrex classification (Beast, Aura, Draconic, Verdant, Aquatic, Crystal, Radiant, Corrupted, Chrono, Astral, Divine, Elemental, Nature, Tech, Spirit, Extraterrestrial, Humanoid-Noid, Creature, Unknown-Void, Ultramax).

The immediate threat is **The Seers.** On the surface they look like a human-supremacist cult stealing one **Gemshard** from each of the 10 Gemlords. Beneath the surface, they are running a much larger ritual — see §3.

## 3 · THE REAL PLOT (V3.5 story lock — critical)

**The Seers are not just stealing Gemshards. That's Phase 1 of a much larger plan.**

Throughout the game the Seers are **secretly kidnapping both Zyrex and humanoid civilians from every district.** Disappearances happen quietly. Villagers explain them away — wild Zyrex attacks, accidents, people running away, isolated crimes. **Only late in the game does the player realize every disappearance is connected.**

The victims are being **sacrificed to Xenoxil.** He uses their life-energy to strengthen himself and to prepare **Orryx and Ophira** for ascension to pseudo-immortality.

**The Seers' full plan:**
1. Recover all ten Gemshards.
2. Use the sacrifices to elevate Orryx and Ophira toward pseudo-immortality.
3. Defeat every Gemlord.
4. Seize each Fathergem.
5. Rule Zyraxis permanently.

**The Gemlords are not the enemy.** They are the final obstacle to Xenoxil's conquest. The player's job is to stop the ritual before the Fathergem seizures begin.

## 4 · The three Seer leaders (updated V3.5)

### XENOXIL — the Fleshlord (Tier VII Pseudoimmortal, founder)

Originally the **Fleshlord of another world.** Does not value humanoid life the way native Zyraxis civilizations do. Views life as biological fuel. Calm, logical, emotionally detached. Rarely raises his voice. His greatest weapon is his complete lack of compassion. His signature line:

> "If a thousand lives forge one eternal guardian, then those thousand lives served their purpose."

Scenes should be quiet, monochrome, unhurried. No music swell. He is more terrifying at a whisper.

### ORRYX — the Reluctant Believer (Tier V Apex)

**Not purely evil.** Genuinely believes eternal leadership is necessary to prevent future wars. Sees Xenoxil's promise of immortality as a necessary sacrifice. Wants order. His tragedy: he slowly compromises his morality in pursuit of stability. Give him doubt beats. Give him a moment where he almost defects.

### OPHIRA — the Prophetess (Tier V Apex)

Treats Xenoxil almost like a prophet. Believes the sacrifices are sacred. Her followers **willingly participate.** They believe they are creating a perfect future. Her scenes carry a **quiet religious atmosphere** — choirs, hymns, echoes, unsettling serenity. Beauty as horror.

## 7 · The Gemlord Pact (canon)

Long ago the Ten Gemlords made a sacred agreement: they would remain within their sanctuaries and **never directly intervene in mortal affairs unless absolutely necessary.** Their self-imposed isolation protects both their Fathergems and the balance of Zyraxis.

**This is why the Gemlords don't fight the Seers themselves.** Their sanctuaries stay sealed through the entire main story. Only after all 10 Gemshards are restored do the sanctuaries open — and even then, Gemlord duels are **optional** post-endgame content, not story fights.

The Pact is a lore anchor — use it whenever the player asks "why don't the Gemlords just kill the Seers?" Every Gemlord's silence is a covenant, not indifference.

## 8 · World Consequence System

**Every district must contain evidence of quiet disappearances:**
- Missing family members
- Abandoned homes
- Closed businesses
- Orphaned children
- Unfinished projects
- Frightened villagers

**After defeating each Seer Commander, the district visibly begins recovering:**
- Shops reopen
- NPCs return home
- Children begin playing again
- Roads become safer
- Dialogue becomes hopeful

**The player is restoring civilization, not just defeating bosses.** This is the central emotional payoff of the game. Sidequests are the primary vehicle for surfacing both the decay AND the recovery — critical-path exposition dumps are forbidden.

## 9 · Recurring characters

### THE QUIET CHILD
- Never introduced. Never explains themselves.
- Appears near shrines and sacred locations across every district.
- Silently watches the player.
- Carries the same **worn stuffed Zyrex toy.**
- Never asks for help. Appears in impossible places.
- **Late-game arc:** the child's parents were taken by the Seers. The child thought the player worked alongside the Seers (because the player enters Seer HQs). Challenges the player to a battle. After losing, finally speaks.
- **Post-Xenoxil:** realizes the player fought to save Zyraxis. Arc ends in **forgiveness, not revenge.** They represent hope surviving grief.

### DAD'S FORMER MENTOR
- One of the oldest living scholars on Zyraxis.
- **Personally knew EGNELLAHC** before Egnellahc split into what became **Oathane (IX) and Oatheus (X)**.
- Speaks in fragments and forgotten memories. Never fully explains.
- Signature tone: *"People speak of the Ninth and the Tenth. I remember when there was only One."*
- Primary source for ancient lore: Fleshlord backstory, Egnellahc split, Verdant Awakening as felt on Zyraxis, Gemlord Pact origin.
- **Post-story optional SUPERBOSS.** Not hostile — he just wants to test whether the next generation is worthy of inheriting Zyraxis. Team = Tier VII/VIII rarities up to Lv 100.

### THE WANDERING MERCHANT
- Comedic. Constantly drinking. Always telling ridiculous stories that contradict each other (some are true).
- Somehow survives impossible situations. Banned from several towns for specific reasons (each ban a running gag).
- Still welcomed everywhere because he brings news, rare items, and a laugh.
- Sells escalating inventory as districts unlock. Some rare items require hearing a story out.
- Never explains where he goes between towns. Ambiguous whether he's more than he seems.

### THE JOURNALIST — the player's childhood best friend
- Runs an **intergalactic radio broadcast** known throughout the Aethryx Expanse.
- Travels alongside the player's journey (parallel path, not party member).
- Interviews **villagers, Rizers, scientists, travelers, merchants, survivors** in every district.
- Player helps **repair transmission equipment** and **complete broadcasts** throughout the game.
- Broadcasts become the **living record of Zyraxis during the Seer crisis.**
- As the game progresses, **listeners from other worlds tune in.** By the endgame, the player's actions are known across the wider Aethryx Expanse.

### PLAYER-FACING NAMED NPCs already in the game
- **Yara** — Seer scout in Malezor; also YARA-PRIME (Malezor's Commander) inside the HQ. Defection arc = open.
- **Jax** — cocky rival Rizer. Bark-partnered. Recurs as the player's mirror across districts.
- **Quinn** — desert wanderer. Dunechitter/Pebblequil pair. Nomad wisdom voice.
- **Myara** — Astromancer's ally from home. Softer counterpart to the Seers.
- **Astromancer Lyra** — good-faction cosmic reader. Hometown.
- **Beastwarden / Voidkeeper / Foreman** — town-hall Rizers per district. Gift-givers.

## 10 · Sidequest philosophy (LOCKED)

**Every sidequest must answer at least ONE of these five questions:**

1. **Who lives here?**
2. **What happened here?**
3. **What is changing here?**
4. **Who is lying?**
5. **What does the player become?**

**Main story stays focused and straightforward.** The depth of Zyraxis emerges through its optional content. Sidequests are not filler — they are how the world becomes worth saving.

**Categories to cover:**
- Local community stories
- Comedy
- Ecology
- Moral decisions
- Multi-district quest chains
- Ancient lore
- Horror and anomalies (Netharion especially)
- Mystery investigations
- Legendary encounters
- Postgame world restoration

**Anti-patterns to avoid:**
- Pure fetch quests with no character reveal
- "Kill 10 wild Zyrex" busywork
- Isolated one-off quests unlinked to a district's disappearance thread OR a recurring character
- Rewards without meaning (currency drops with no context)

**Prefer:**
- Chains over one-shots (a Malezor quest paying off in Zarvane or later)
- Consequences over rewards (the world reacts)
- Character reveal over loot

## 11 · Terminology + tone (do not violate)

- **Zyrex** = the creatures. Plural is also "Zyrex."
- **Rizer** = trainer. Player class.
- **Gemlord** = one of the Ten. Never call them Zyrex.
- **Fathergem** = the full core gem inside each Gemlord. Untouchable in main story.
- **Gemshard** = a chip of a Fathergem. Ten total, one per district.
- **Zysphere** = capture ball. Clicked open (not thrown). Reverse-cosmic beam extracts astralite DNA over ~1.5s → binary PERFECT SYNC or ASTRALITES RESIST. Manufactured by **THARDUN corporation** in Thardin.
- **Astralite** = cosmic-genetic material every Zyrex carries.
- **Fleshlord** = an off-world title, XENOXIL's origin role. Ancient civilizations remember the term.
- **Egnellahc** = the pre-split figure whose essence became Oathane + Oatheus. Referenced by Dad's mentor.
- **The Verdant Awakening** = cosmological event that seeded all Viridian creatures. Zyraxis-native Zyrex are ancestor/echo strains of this.
- **The Gemlord Pact** = the Ten's ancient covenant of non-intervention.

**Tone:** mid-to-high fantasy, cosmic-sci-fi at the edges, warm at home, ominous in Seer scenes, serene-horror in Ophira scenes, monochrome-quiet in Xenoxil scenes, wondrous in caves and shrines. **Never modern slang. No emoji.** No corporate-speak in dialog.
