BEFORE YOU BEGIN

What this book is — and why the version number on the cover matters more than it looks.

You play a Rizer: a bond-master who binds creatures called Zyrex using astralite-sync artifacts called Zyspheres. You begin in a bronze cottage in Malezor, District I, with a father who studies these creatures for a living and a mother whose name still opens doors eight districts away. Everything after that is yours to find.

This book covers the world, the creature systems, combat, the bond, and the shape of the journey. It does not tell you where the secrets are — that is deliberate, and Part 07 explains why.

The version tag

Every build carries a three-part stamp:

VERSION FORMAT · LOCKED CANON

BETA V{game}.{gamedex}.{codex} — the game build, the Gamedex, and the Master Codex, marching together. This edition is BETA V7.5.16: RP7 · Gamedex 5 · Codex 16. The "BETA" prefix drops at 1.0 launch.

The three numbers are not decoration. The Master Codex and the Gamedex are canonically synchronized: what is in one is in the other, and no canon lock changes one without changing the other in the same bump. When the world changes, this book changes with it.

The games are locked canon

The saga reaches its audiences through three doors — games, film, and the ten-book arc — and the games are the door most people walk through first. Because of that, the game's mechanics, roster and geography are treated as canonical fact, not adaptable creative liberty.

CANON INTENT

What the game teaches is what happens on Zyraxis. Every card in the TCG is a real Zyrex at its canonical stats. Every district in the RPG is the actual place. The Codex and the game roster share one Zyrex table, one type chart, one stat system.

PART 01

THE WORLD

Zyraxis is the ninth planet of the Genesis System and the Spirit anchor of the Aethryx Expanse. Gems crystallized here first.

The Aethryx Expanse is a twenty-eight-planet system orbiting the star Aenor. Zyraxis is Planet #9 — the Ax-9 Astryx Soul CORE, the Spirit-substrate anchor of the entire system, and the origin world of every bonding technique that exists anywhere in the Expanse.

Long before anyone walked it, the Mothergem shattered here. Its ten shards embedded in the crust, and around each shard a Gemlord crystallized and claimed the land above it. The War of the Gemlords that followed ended not in conquest but in the Gemlord Pact — overwhelming power restrained by neutrality, free will, and deliberate non-intervention.

THE PACT

The Gemlords protect Zyraxis most powerfully by refusing to own it.

How time works here

Earth and the Aethryx Expanse share one universal timeline. Time does not run faster here; the Expanse simply formed billions of years earlier. What differs is the calendar, set by the repeating Aenor-Zoryth resonance pulse:

The extraordinary lifespans you will hear about are not time distortion. They are an astralite property — concentrated matrices and reduced biological entropy. Ordinary mortals get decades. Enhanced humanoids get centuries. Astrums measure their lives in millions of years, and the Immortals do not age at all.

PART 02

YOU ARE A RIZER

A Rizer is not a fighter. A Rizer is a bond-master — and the game measures you separately from the creatures you carry.

Your progression and your Zyrex's progression are two different tracks. Zyrex gain levels. You gain Rizer Level — earned from chests opened, grunts and Commanders defeated, sidequests completed, story beats reached. There is no ceiling; the curve keeps scaling past Lv 100 for the post-endgame Gemlord duels.

RIZER LEVEL CURVE

rizerXpForNext(lv) = 100 + (lv × 50) + floor(lv² × 0.6)

Your HUD shows it as ★ R.Lv N · XP/NEED. Rizer Level gates merchant stock, bond lessons, and sphere tiers — it is the key that opens the rest of the game.

The Zysphere

Zyrex are bound with Zyspheres — spherical astralite-DNA sync artifacts, mass-manufactured by Thardun Corporate in District IX. They are the industrialized descendants of ancient Zyraxian ritual craft.

You click a sphere open at close range. It locks onto the target's astralite signature and runs a binding sequence of about a second and a half. The result is binary — there is no catch percentage to grind against:

  • PERFECT SYNC — the Zyrex is bound.
  • RESIST — the sphere is consumed and the target flees.
CANON LOCK · THE ONLY WAY IN

Wild Zyrex are caught with a Zysphere and by nothing else. No trap, no item, no battle outcome and no quest reward binds a wild Zyrex in its place. The sphere is the single route from the wild into your party — which is why sphere tier is the most consequential thing you can spend money on.

The three meters

Your HUD carries three parallel resource meters, and each one has exactly one replenishment loop. Nothing cross-feeds. This is the single most important thing to internalize about the overworld.

❤
HEALTH

Your own vitality — separate from your Zyrex's HP. Drains from environmental damage, enemy attacks and direct Souldrift ticks. At zero you faint and the party retreats to the nearest bed or save point. Restored by FOOD only. Bread, fruit, cooked meals, Zarvane oasis produce, meats, vegetables — meal quality sets the restore amount.

⚡
LIGHTNING

Baseline stamina. Fuels sprint, punch, kick and other physical exertion. Empty means walking only. Restored by FAEDUST — the refined form of wild fairies you catch with the Faenet.

◆
BLUE DIAMOND

The magic economy. Every A3 Astral and A4 Ultimate attack costs diamond; A1 and A2 are free. Run dry and those two slots gray out until you refill. Restored by FISH.

THE NO-CROSS-REPLENISH RULE

Food does not restore lightning. Fairies do not restore health. Fish do not restore either. Three meters, three gather loops, three reasons to actually explore. Districts differ by what they are rich in — Malezor has forests and farms, Zarvane has oasis water, the Wild Arm has fairies.

Fishing and fairy-catching

Fishing runs at any water tile with a rod equipped. You get a five-second window; an X icon flashes at a random moment inside it; you have one second to press. Hit it and you land a fish. Miss it — or jump early — and the window resets. It is short, it is reaction-based, and there is no pattern to memorize.

Fairy-catching requires the Faenet. No net, no fairies. Wild fairies live in grass patches and fae-tiles across every district, at densities that vary by region. What you catch feeds two very different appetites:

THE FAE TRADE-OFF · ONE POOL, TWO GOALS

Fae refine into faedust to refill your lightning meter — and fae also accumulate toward the Celestryx craft. Both draw on the same stock. Every fae you burn on convenience is one that does not go toward the recipe.

The recipe unlocks at 10 fae — that is the moment you learn it exists. Actually crafting a Celestryx takes 100 fae at the science table.

Rizer XP is spendable. You bank RXP from combat, exploration, achievements, story beats and sidequests, then spend it as points at the level-up screen. Where those points go is described in gem colors — one color language that runs through Zyrex stats, your stats, the UI meters, the shop categories and the dialogue.

Your team, and where the rest go

You carry six Zyrex. The seventh and beyond route automatically to PC Storage, accessed from the computer in your room — except the computer starts inactive.

To switch it on you need the Computer Part, a cosmic-substrate component salvaged from a former Rizer's machine, sitting in one of the locked houses of Zarvane (District II). Bring it home, install it, and every Rizer Center terminal on the planet becomes a swap point.

Where you come from

Your father is a leading beastologist. Your mother is Head Nurse of Baelgor. The family emigrated from Baelgor in District VIII to Malezor in District I so your father could study Zyrex in the ruby-cavern ecosystem up close. You start in the Bronze Cottage — the new house — with a father who understands the creatures and a mother whose district connections come back around in the mid-game.

PART 03

ZYREX

Every Zyrex has a tier, up to three types, an archetype, and five stats that always sum to the same number for its tier.

Tiers and the stat pool

Zyrex are ranked in tiers I through X, running from Basic up to God class. The tier is not a label — it is a budget. Every species' base stat pool is exactly:

THE T×333 RULE

STAT POOL = TIER × 333 — spread across HP · ATK · DEF · SPD · SPC.

Tier I gets 333. Tier V gets 1,665. Tier X gets 3,330. A glass-cannon Tier V and a bulky Tier V hold the same budget — they just spend it differently.

Printed cards show a Zyrex at its MAX form, at level = tier × 10. A Tier III card is Lv 30; a Tier V card is Lv 50. Stats scale linearly from 25% at Lv 1 up to 100% at max, capped at the canonical codex figures.

HOW EFFECTIVENESS WORKS

The chart is a 3/3 symmetric structure: every one of the twenty types is strong against exactly three others and weak against exactly three others, with no mutual counter-pairs anywhere. Sixty directed edges, no type universally dominant, and no type that can be safely ignored.

The two families interlock rather than stack. Each of the expansion ten beats exactly one of the original ten and is beaten by exactly one — so the newer types did not sit on top of the older ones, they wove into them.

STACKING & CANCELLATION

A defender carries more than one type, so more than one relationship can apply at once. Both of a defender's types vulnerable is the heaviest hit; one vulnerable and one neutral is the ordinary super-effective case; both resistant is the hardest wall in the game.

Cancellation: where one of a defender's types is vulnerable and another resists the same attack, the two cancel and the hit lands neutral. A same-typed Zyrex has nothing to cancel with — which is why pure typing hits hardest and folds hardest.

Zyrex at Tier VI and above may carry a third type, which unlocks access to multiple archetype modifiers. Tri-typing is where the late game's strategic depth lives.

★ ULTIMATE — THE 21ST TYPE

Ultimate is not part of the chart. It sits above it, reserved exclusively for Tier X God-class cards, single-type only, and it cannot be picked, crafted, evolved into or earned.

Incoming: 1× from all twenty standard types. No weaknesses. Outgoing: 3× against every type. Status: immune to all five. Capture: uncatchable by Zysphere, Zycube or Zyramid — bondable only through cosmic ceremony. Stat pool: exempt from T×333; both carriers stand at 999 in every stat, 4,995 total.

Two beings carry it: ANCIUXOR and ULTHARIS. The register is closed — no future being joins it, however powerful. Meeting an Ultimate carrier is a ceremony, not a fight you can win.

PART 04

COMBAT

Four moves. Two of them are safe. Two of them gamble. One shared combat vocabulary across the card game and the RPG.

The four slots

Every Zyrex has exactly four moves, in fixed slots, and all four are attacks — there is no support-only slot in this game. Physical cards show them in a 2×2 grid; so does the RPG's combat UI.

A1
LIGHT

Quick hit. Straight damage, no status chance. Fastest slot, cheapest, highest PP. Unlocks Lv 1.

A2
HEAVY

Heavy hit. Straight damage, no status chance. Slower, hits harder, mid PP. Unlocks Lv 15.

A3
ASTRAL

Quick hit with a kicker — damage plus a light chance of applying one of the five statuses. Unlocks Lv 30.

A4
ULTIMATE

The finisher. Heavy damage plus a heavy status chance. Lowest PP, highest cost. Unlocks Lv 60.

The grid scales on two axes at once. Odd slots are quick, even slots are heavy. The top row is pure damage; the bottom row gambles on effects. Every Zyrex is therefore a mixed toolkit by construction — two safe plays and two gambles. There are no lock-the-enemy-without-hitting-them builds, because there is no slot that would allow one.

WHAT IT COSTS

In the RPG, A3 and A4 burn Blue Diamond ◆. A1 and A2 are free. At zero diamond the bottom row grays out and you fight with the top row until you find water. In the card game, attacks draw on the nine-gem economy — nine gems per side, no regeneration, so every early trade costs you a late-game option.

Whether a status lands

Damage always lands. The status is a separate roll, and the roll is decided by one number:

STATUS EFFECT CHANCE

CHANCE = attacker's SPECIAL ÷ maximum SPECIAL for that tier

A Tier V Zyrex with Special 200 against a tier maximum of 400 rolls at 0.50. Special is the status-magic stat: build into it and your A3/A4 kit becomes reliable; ignore it and those slots are just damage with a lottery ticket attached.

Because the denominator is the tier's own ceiling, the system is naturally normalized — a Tier I at its tier maximum procs as reliably as a Tier X at its maximum. And because Special grows with level, a young Zyrex's A3/A4 is genuinely weaker than a mature one's. Maturity gates status power.

The five statuses

One status per stat. Four of them are fixed cuts — applied once, at full magnitude, and they do not tick further. One of them is a drain, and it is the only status that can kill.

StatusStatMagnitudeKindDuration
GRIDFREEZESPC−50%Fixed cut3 turns
UNDERSHOCKSPD−40%Fixed cut3 turns
OVERCOOKATK−30%Fixed cut3 turns

Gridfreeze is the heaviest cut in the game, and it deserves the reputation. It halves Special — which does not only reduce the target's magical damage, it halves their own future proc rate through the chance formula. Landing it multiplies your control over the whole fight.

Souldrift is the one to fear. Ten percent of maximum HP every turn, no expiry timer, and it does not stop when the battle does — it keeps ticking in the overworld. Ten turns from full is death. Cure it, kill the source, or lose the Zyrex.

Statuses stack — one per stat, five slots maximum — and each runs its own timer independently, so relief arrives in cascade unless the attacker keeps re-applying.

EVERY STATUS HAS A MIRROR BUFF

Ignite +30% ATK ↔ Overcook · Bulwark +20% DEF ↔ Psychache · Slipstream +40% SPD ↔ Undershock · Astralwake +50% SPC ↔ Gridfreeze · Reforge +10%/turn HP ↔ Souldrift.

Astralwake works both directions: it raises your Special, so it also raises your own A3/A4 proc rate while it holds.

Curing, fainting, reviving

  • Potions do not cure status. They restore HP. That is all they do.
  • Potions do nothing at all on a KO'd Zyrex. Only a Revival Stem or bed-sleep brings one back.
  • There are five antidotes, one per status. No universal cure exists. Anti-Overcook will not touch Undershock.
  • Faint and revive is the reset. A revived Zyrex comes back clean — full HP, no statuses, whatever put it down.
  • Revival Stem comes from your mother's plant or a tree shake. Full HP and a clean slate in one item — which is exactly why it is rare.

At the card table

The TCG runs the same four slots and the same type chart on paper, plus two rules of its own.

The Gamble. At any point in your turn you may commit a Zyrex to a high-risk escalation of its next move. Land it and damage doubles. Fail it and that Zyrex is stunned. It is the rule that rewards reading your opponent rather than reading the math.

Revivals. Under specific conditions — certain Rizer Specials, certain items, certain endgame effects — a fallen Zyrex returns at reduced HP. Rare enough to feel like an event, never automatic.

PART 05

THE BOND

The strongest Rizers are not the ones holding the strongest Zyrex. They are the ones who sync deepest with the partner who chose to stand beside them.

A Zysphere is not a container. It holds a stabilized astralite matrix linked to the Zyrex bound inside it. Ordinarily, activating the sphere releases that Zyrex to fight on its own. But an advanced Zyraxian humanoid can do something else entirely: keep the Zyrex inside and channel its matrix through their own body.

That is Bonding. Instead of the standard summon, you click and keep holding. The Zyrex never materializes. Its matrix partially synchronizes with yours, and you perform attacks that belong to it — not as a borrowed spell, but as a shared technique. Both partners are conscious throughout. Both feel it. These are Bond Moves, and they are expressions of partnership rather than magic.

TRUST CANNOT BE MANUFACTURED

Not every Rizer can do this, and no amount of technology substitutes. Bonding requires a genuine emotional and spiritual connection — the two matrices must synchronize willingly. Without that mutual trust the sphere never establishes resonance and the Bond Move simply does not happen.

Why humanoids are different

Any Zyrex whose primary type is Humanoid is a Zyraxian humanoid rather than a conventional creature. They carry biological astralite matrices much like a human's, and they express that energy through conscious control — martial arts, spellcasting, weapon technique, disciplined manipulation. Beast, nature and spirit Zyrex carry denser matrices that manifest instinctively and physically. That difference is exactly why humanoid Zyraxians are the natural bonding partners for humanoid Rizers.

Channeling beyond the body

The greatest Bond Masters discover that astralite energy is not confined to a body. Rather than channel the Zyrex through themselves, they can transfer bond energy into external objects — weapons, relics, ceremonial artifacts, enchanted equipment. The object briefly becomes an extension of the bond itself. Mechanically this is the late-game unlock that opens legendary equipment slots, signature-weapon builds, and relic-empowered attacks drawing on both partner and artifact.

Prismshards

Among the oldest relics in existence are Prismshards — vessels deliberately crafted to preserve, amplify or stabilize astralite energy. A master can temporarily synchronize a bonded Zyrex through one, reaching abilities ordinary bonding cannot produce. Some scholars trace them to prehistoric Zyraxis, carved from Netharion amethyst or Malezor ruby by first-generation ritualists. Others insist certain Prismshards predate the Aenor Eruption itself. Nobody knows, and the mystery is part of the power.

THE PRISMS ARE NOT LOOT

The sixteen Astralite Prisms are foundational pieces of cosmic history — each unique in identity, rules and consequence. They are never entries in a rare-drop table, and no player collects all sixteen by grinding. If a Prism appears in your game it is a canonical event.

The Key of Anciuxor, the sole Tier IV Prism, is the most consequential artifact in the entire saga. It will never be a collectible.

CANON INTENT

Power is earned through connection, not possession.

The Novarian Challenge

A nine-year planetary bracket held in Novarius's honor. You field a Faction of 9 — a mixed roster of Zyrex and Rizers representing your home district. You qualify at Malezor's arena, then play one match per year cycle across all ten districts, and the championship is fought in Korathen under Oatheus's terminal-Prism arena. The prize is the Imperial Coin, struck in Korathen and bearing Oatheus's mark. The Grand Champion inherits Novarius's mantle as the era's first Beast Master.

The world remembers what you did

Every district visibly carries the disappearance crisis. Before you defeat that district's Seer Commander you will find missing family members, abandoned homes, shuttered businesses, orphaned children, unfinished work, frightened people. Afterward, shops reopen. Neighbors come home. Children play in the road. Dialogue turns hopeful.

Four people you will keep meeting

  • The Quiet Child. Silent, carrying a worn stuffed Zyrex toy, appearing near shrines in every district — sometimes in places a child could not have reached. There is an answer to this, and it takes the whole game to arrive.
  • Your father's old mentor. One of the oldest living scholars on Zyraxis, who knew Egnellahc before the Ninth and Tenth split. Your best source of lore, and a post-story superboss fielding Tier VII–VIII rarities at Lv 100.
  • The Wandering Merchant. Always drinking, banned from several towns, welcome in all of them. His stock grows with your Rizer Level. It is genuinely unclear whether he is only a merchant.
  • The Journalist. Your childhood best friend, running an intergalactic radio broadcast heard across the Expanse. She travels parallel to you, interviewing villagers in every district — and by the end, what you have done is known cosmically.

Why the sidequests are not filler

Every sidequest in this game must answer at least one of five questions: Who lives here? What happened here? What is changing here? Who is lying? What does the player become? The main story stays focused and straightforward. Depth is optional — and it is where the world becomes worth saving.

▶ SPOILERS · THE STORY BEHIND THE DISAPPEARANCES

The Seers — Xenoxil's cult — are taking civilians and Zyrex from every district. Villagers assume wild attacks or accidents. Only late does it become clear that every disappearance across the whole planet is one operation.

The victims are sacrificed to Xenoxil, who uses the life-energy to strengthen himself while preparing Orryx and Ophira for pseudo-immortality. His plan runs in five stages: recover all ten Gemshards; elevate Orryx and Ophira through sacrifice; defeat every Gemlord; seize each Fathergem; rule Zyraxis permanently.

Note what that means. The Gemlords are not the enemy of this story — they are the final obstacle standing between Xenoxil and the world.

The Gemlord respect-fights. Optional post-endgame content, unlocked only after all ten Gemshards are restored and the sanctuaries open. Each Gemlord offers a duel — not hostile, not required — a chance for a Lv 100+ Rizer to test their mastery. Oatheus in Korathen, Tier X Ultramax, is the last of them and the Pact's final seal.

The Anciuxor home-cycle. There is a ritual sequence, executed in order at your home base, that bonds Anciuxor. Interact with the statue once the main endgame clears. Pray at it ten times. A statue part appears in your inventory. Receive your mother's blessing in the kitchen and your father's warning in the study. Burn the part in the fireplace. Anciuxor grants itself to you.

Anciuxor's tail carries the Key of Anciuxor. Bonding one bonds the other — which is the canonical path to Prismsynch communion with Ultharis.

PART 07

FIELD GUIDE

Ten things worth knowing before you leave Malezor.

01

Lead with Gridfreeze

It is the heaviest cut in the game and the only one that compounds. Halving a target's Special halves their magical damage and halves their own chance of landing anything back at you. Land it first and the next three turns belong to you.

02

Never leave town on an empty tank

A3 and A4 burn Blue Diamond, and diamond comes from fish and nothing else. Walk into a Commander fight dry and you are swinging A1 and A2 for the whole encounter. Fish first.

03

Souldrift follows you out of the fight

Every other status clears when the battle ends. Souldrift does not — it keeps draining 10% a turn in the overworld. If you walk away from a fight with it active, you are on a timer. Cure it or get to a bed.

04

Fight above your weight

The level-difference clamp pays up to 1.8× for beating something stronger than you, and as little as 0.5× for farming things far beneath you. Grinding down is the slowest way to level in this game.

05

Special is a build decision

Your status chance is your Special divided by your tier's maximum Special. A Zyrex with a low Special is not "balanced" — it is a Zyrex whose bottom row rarely does anything. Decide early whether a given partner is your damage or your control.

06

Do not spend all your fae

Faedust refills your stamina, and it is tempting. But the Celestryx recipe costs 100 fae from the same pool. Every convenient sprint is a withdrawal from that account.

07

Get the Computer Part early

It sits in a locked house in Zarvane, District II. Until you install it, every Zyrex past your sixth is stranded. Going after it early changes how you play the next eight districts.

08

Potions are not first aid

They restore HP. They do not cure status, and they do nothing whatsoever on a KO'd Zyrex. For that you need a Revival Stem, a bed, or the right one of the five antidotes — and there is no universal cure to fall back on.

09

Visit every town hall

The nine Elder quests scale from +1,000 to +4,500 Rizer XP, and Rizer Level is what gates spheres, merchant stock and bond lessons. They are the highest-value optional content in the base game.

10

The ladder matters more than the tier

A Tier I on the Epsilon ladder finishes at Tier VIII. A Tier I on the Alpha ladder finishes at Tier III. What a Zyrex is now tells you much less than what path it walks.

ON SECRETS · POLICY LOCK

This book will never tell you where to go or what to bring for an easter egg. Locked cosmetics read only "Locked." Hints stay evocative and stay vague. Discovery is a community mechanic here — the answers are meant to spread between players, not to be printed.

This edition is stamped BETA V7.5.16 — RP7, Gamedex 5, Codex 16.
