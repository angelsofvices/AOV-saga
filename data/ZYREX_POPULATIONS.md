## THE ANCHOR LAW

Every district names one NATIVE species that is guaranteed to be there. A bonded
wild is marked gone forever, so without this every district is a depleting
resource — catch Baelgor's four Veridrax and Baelgor has none again, permanently.
If an anchor drops below its floor the ground grows it back, one head per 8 real
minutes, scattered near its native tile. Anchors are all T3 or lower so there is
always something present you can actually bond with at the band you arrive in.
