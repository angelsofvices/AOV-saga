# RP7 Main Story Progression Draft

## Canonical Quest Structure

- Each district contains its own main-mission chain and side-quest ecosystem.
- District Elders give the main missions.
- Local NPCs give side quests, character stories, experiments, faction requests, and district-advantage quests.
- Main missions advance the struggle against the Seers and eventually unlock the district's Gemlord trial.
- Side quests expand district lore and may provide practical advantages without replacing required story progression.

## Core Story Spine

The Seers are not merely raiding settlements. Under Xenoxil, the Flesh Lord, they are destabilizing the ten districts so they can seize Zyraxis as one connected system. Orryx and Ophira lead the visible campaign while Xenoxil directs the larger ritual. The Seers siphon Astral energy, corrupt mortals into Mori and Daemon, exploit the missing Gemlords Oathane and Oatheus, and attempt to replace the authority that once held Zyraxis together.

Rizer begins as a low-level youth without a Zyrex. His journey develops across three connected arcs:

1. **The Young Bonder:** Rizer receives his first Zyrex, builds a faction, and earns the trust of district Elders.
2. **The Awakened Rizer:** Azurel unlocks Rizer's S1 power. After Rizer and his allies defeat Orryx and Ophira for the first time, the Seers kidnap his parents. His anger destabilizes the power Azurel awakened. Rakoron then bonds directly with Rizer, helps him control that rage, and unlocks S2.
3. **The Defender of Zyraxis:** Rizer unites the districts, exposes the purpose of the Seer occupation, reaches Korathen, and stops Xenoxil's takeover of the planet.

## Faction Growth Across the Journey

Rizer continually meets potential NPC allies and wild Zyrex while traveling through the ten districts. His faction is not filled automatically through story progression. It expands through demonstrated trust, exploration, quest completion, and player growth.

### NPC Ally Recruitment

Important NPCs begin as acquaintances, rivals, guides, specialists, or temporary mission partners. Each has an individual relationship bond with Rizer. Helping them through main missions, personal side quests, dialogue choices, shared battles, and district crises increases that bond.

An NPC becomes an unlockable faction member only when the player satisfies that character's combination of requirements:

- Meet the NPC through exploration or story progression.
- Complete the NPC's introductory or personal quest requirements.
- Reach the NPC's required bond threshold.
- Reach any required RXP level or expedition milestone.
- Resolve any loyalty, faction, or story conflict preventing recruitment.

Some allies join early, while stronger or more influential allies require longer multi-district relationships. Recruitment quests should reveal why the character chooses Rizer rather than treating the ally as a simple reward.

### Wild Zyrex Recruitment

Wild Zyrex appear throughout district expeditions. Encountering a powerful Zyrex does not automatically mean Rizer is ready to add it to his faction. Stronger Zyrex become bondable as the player proves greater control, experience, and understanding of Zyrex.

Wild Zyrex access scales through:

- **Rizer Bond:** Higher Player–Rizer Bond improves Zysphere effectiveness and proves that the player and Rizer can act as one.
- **RXP Level:** Prevents inexperienced players from commanding Zyrex beyond their current mastery.
- **Expedition Progress:** Reaching deeper, more dangerous areas reveals stronger species, forms, and encounter pools.
- **Quest Completion:** Helping local NPCs, restoring habitats, solving district problems, and completing Zyrex-related quests can make rare Zyrex appear or become receptive.
- **District Progression:** Certain species remain unavailable until the Elder's main-mission chain opens their territory.

The existing Zysphere rule remains in force: Player–Rizer Bond increases capture effectiveness from a **5% baseline** toward a **95% maximum**. Encounter access, capture chance, and command eligibility are related but separate checks; finding or catching a powerful Zyrex does not necessarily mean it can immediately be deployed.

### Four Progression Axes

| Axis | What it represents | Primary unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **RXP** | Player/Rizer combat and mastery level | Command capacity, combat techniques, higher-tier deployment |
| **Bond** | Trust between Player and Rizer, NPC allies, or specific Zyrex | Recruitment, Zysphere effectiveness, advanced cooperation |
| **Expeditions** | Exploration of dangerous district regions | New encounter pools, hidden areas, rare Zyrex, materials |
| **Quests** | Service to residents and progress against district threats | Allies, habitat changes, special encounters, faction advantages |

No single axis should replace the others. A player who power-levels RXP without building relationships should not gain every ally. A player with high bond but little expedition progress should not encounter the strongest wild Zyrex. A player who explores widely but ignores district quests may discover rare creatures without having the knowledge or permission needed to bond with them.

### Recommended Recruitment States

Every recruitable NPC and Zyrex can move through a shared state model:

1. **Unknown** — Not yet encountered.
2. **Discovered** — Added to the player's journal or regional knowledge.
3. **Connected** — Bond or eligibility progression has begun.
4. **Eligible** — All recruitment gates except the final quest, battle, or capture action are satisfied.
5. **Faction Member** — Available for faction assignment.
6. **Trusted/Veteran** — Additional bond unlocks advanced moves, passive abilities, dialogue, or combination tactics.

### Main-Story Scaling

- **Malezor–Zarvane:** Small faction; young allies and low-tier wild Zyrex establish the system.
- **Andrannor–Veridan:** Specialized allies and evolving Zyrex introduce roster-building decisions.
- **Netharion–Vorashil:** Midgame expedition threats require purposeful faction composition; S1 expands Rizer's own combat role.
- **Post-kidnapping Malezor Interlude:** Existing faction bonds determine who helps Rizer through his rage crisis. Rakoron's bond unlocks S2.
- **Xilnar–Baelgor:** Veteran allies, Spirit-capable specialists, and stronger Zyrex become essential against Seer operations.
- **Thardun–Korathen:** The player's accumulated faction becomes the practical proof that Rizer has united Zyraxis. Recruited allies contribute to the final operation according to their expertise and bond level.

### Gemlord Exception

Gemlords do not use ordinary wild-Zyrex recruitment. To add a Gemlord to the faction, Rizer must defeat it in its cave through **Player Faction vs. Enemy** combat, earn its respect, and have Player–Rizer Bond above **80%**. Story circumstances and the ancient non-intervention pact can still limit when or how a Gemlord may be deployed.

## Power Progression — Locked Order

### S1 — Azurel's Awakening

Azurel senses that Rizer possesses dormant Rizer power but lacks the internal flow required to wield it safely. In Vorashil, Azurel uses Sapphire resonance to open and stabilize Rizer's first transformation channel. S1 is founded on perception, clarity, and controlled Aura circulation. It is not fueled by anger.

### The Parents' Kidnapping

Rizer and his allies defeat Seer Chiefs Ophira and Orryx for the first time only after S1 has been unlocked. The defeat forces the Seers to escalate. Rizer's mother and father are kidnapped because of their knowledge, their connection to Rizer, and the strategic value of breaking Rizer emotionally.

### S2 — Rakoron's Bond

Rizer's fury after the kidnapping causes S1 to surge beyond its safe limits. He returns to Malezor and seeks Rakoron. Rakoron does not simply grant him more strength; the Rubylord forms a direct bond with Rizer and teaches him to survive the rage without becoming ruled by it. Building upon the stable inner channel Azurel created, Rakoron unlocks S2: a stronger, primal combat form powered by disciplined fury, protective instinct, and the will to survive.

S2 therefore requires both Gemlords:

- **Azurel provides the controlled foundation.**
- **Rakoron teaches Rizer to command the rage layered over it.**

# District Main-Story Progression

## I — Malezor, Beastlands

**Gemlord:** Rakoron, the Rubylord  
**Story function:** Beginning, first Zyrex, survival, and the foundation of Rizer's bond philosophy.

Rizer temporarily relocates to Malezor with his family for his father's Beast-type research. He begins without a Zyrex and is treated as an outsider who has not proven he can survive the Beastlands.

### Main-mission draft

1. **Stranger in the Beastlands** — The Malezor Elder introduces the district's laws and sends Rizer to learn its territorial customs.
2. **The Beastmaster's Study** — Rizer assists his father with local Zyrex research and learns that wild behavior is becoming unnaturally aggressive.
3. **The First Bond** — At the Zyrex lab, Rizer receives his first partner Zyrex and learns the fundamentals of Player–Rizer–Zyrex cooperation.
4. **Predators Without Territory** — Rizer investigates displaced Zyrex and discovers evidence that Seer activity is disrupting migration patterns.
5. **Trial of the Fanghall** — Rizer completes a survival and bonding trial to earn Malezor's recognition.
6. **The Rubylord's Cave** — Rizer encounters Rakoron but is not yet ready for a complete personal bond. Rakoron recognizes his potential and permits his journey to continue.
7. **Tracks Beyond Malezor** — Evidence points toward coordinated interference in other districts, unlocking Zarvane.

**Important later return:** Malezor becomes the setting for the S2 awakening after Rizer's parents are kidnapped.

## II — Zarvane, Auralands

**Gemlord:** Ivirium, the Pearlord  
**Story function:** Aura perception, basic Rizer combat, and evidence that the Seers are manipulating district-wide energy flow.

### Main-mission draft

1. **A City Out of Tune** — Zarvane's Elder reveals that citizens and Zyrex are experiencing synchronized emotional disturbances.
2. **Read the Current** — Rizer learns elementary Aura perception and basic personal combat.
3. **The Stolen Frequency** — Seer devices are found siphoning energy from the Resonance Spire.
4. **Silence in the Conservatory** — Rizer protects researchers decoding the Seers' frequency pattern.
5. **Pearl Resonance** — Ivirium tests whether Rizer can distinguish another being's true Aura from an imposed signal.
6. **The Network Beneath the Attacks** — The recovered frequency reveals that the district incidents are connected parts of a planetary operation.

## III — Andrannor, Creaturelands

**Gemlord:** Mutaryn, the Citrinelord  
**Story function:** Evolution, hybrid threats, faction expansion, and proof that the Seers are altering living bodies.

### Main-mission draft

1. **Everything Is Becoming** — Andrannor's Elder reports uncontrolled transformations among local creatures.
2. **The Morphic Menagerie** — Rizer contains an evolutionary chain reaction without harming the affected Zyrex.
3. **Night at CLUB VX** — A Seer courier uses the crowded nightclub to distribute a mutagenic Astralite compound.
4. **The Market Below** — Rizer exposes an illegal operation moving captured or altered creatures through the Chimera Exchange.
5. **Mutaryn's Measure** — Mutaryn tests whether Rizer respects evolution as adaptation rather than as forced improvement.
6. **Flesh-Lord Evidence** — Research links the alterations to Xenoxil, though the Seers' leader remains out of reach.

## IV — Veridan, Naturelands

**Gemlord:** Emeralix, the Emeralord  
**Story function:** Planetary ecosystems, resistance to corruption, and the realization that Zyraxis itself is being prepared for takeover.

### Main-mission draft

1. **The Green Wall Withers** — Veridan's Elder reveals that multiple ecosystems are failing simultaneously.
2. **Seeds That Refuse to Grow** — Rizer investigates corrupted soil and missing specimens at the Seedvault.
3. **Roots Remember** — The living Root Parliament reveals that the disturbance travels through ancient underground channels.
4. **The Hungry Cure** — A healing organism at the Overgrowth Hospice becomes corrupted and begins absorbing patients.
5. **Emeralix's Restoration** — Rizer restores a damaged biome and proves that domination is not the same as stewardship.
6. **A Planet Made Vulnerable** — The Seers are weakening the natural systems that would resist a planetary ritual.

## V — Netharion, Unknownlands

**Gemlord:** Eurakeon, the Amethystlord  
**Story function:** First direct battle with Ophira, forbidden knowledge, and discovery of the takeover ritual.

### Main-mission draft

1. **Enter the Instability Core** — Netharion's Elder warns Rizer that ordinary maps and physical rules cannot be trusted.
2. **The Impossible Record** — The archive contains erased references to the disappearance of Oathane and Oatheus.
3. **Eyes in the Null** — The Null Observatory detects a ritual pattern spanning all ten districts.
4. **The Crooked Captives** — Rizer rescues researchers trapped inside the Crooked House with Seer agents.
5. **Ophira's False Future** — Ophira attempts to break Rizer by showing him possible futures in which his journey destroys his family.
6. **Chief of the Unknown** — Rizer and his allies defeat Ophira for the first time, disrupting one half of the Seers' district-control network.
7. **The Sapphire Signal** — Eurakeon directs Rizer toward Azurel, whose power may prepare him to face Orryx.

## VI — Vorashil, Allelands

**Gemlord:** Azurel, the Sapphirelord  
**Story function:** S1 awakening, first defeat of Orryx, and the turning point that triggers the parents' kidnapping.

### Main-mission draft

1. **Thought Without Words** — Vorashil's Elder requires Rizer to communicate with non-humanoid intelligences before investigating the district crisis.
2. **The Shape Embassy Fracture** — Seer interference causes several alien species to interpret one another as threats.
3. **Many Bodies, One Witness** — The Manybody consciousness reveals that Orryx is constructing the second half of the control network.
4. **Azurel's Inner Current** — Azurel identifies Rizer's dormant potential and subjects him to a perception trial in which force alone guarantees failure.
5. **Sapphire Awakening** — Azurel opens and stabilizes Rizer's transformation channel, unlocking **S1** through clarity, Aura control, and conscious intent.
6. **Orryx and Vorashil** — Rizer uses S1 alongside his faction to defeat Orryx for the first time and destroy the second control anchor.
7. **The Seers Strike Home** — With both Chiefs defeated, Xenoxil orders Rizer's parents kidnapped. Their capture is retaliation, leverage, and part of the Seers' revised plan.

## Interlude — Return to Malezor: The Ruby Rage

This required story interlude occurs after Vorashil and before the full Xilnar campaign.

1. **An Empty Home** — Rizer finds evidence of his parents' abduction and loses control of S1.
2. **Rage Across the Beastlands** — His unstable Aura provokes local predators and endangers his own faction.
3. **Return to the Rubylord** — The Malezor Elder sends Rizer back to Rakoron's cave, knowing ordinary training cannot contain him.
4. **The Predator Within** — Rakoron forces Rizer to confront the difference between protective anger and blind violence.
5. **Bond of Ruby** — Rakoron forms a direct bond with Rizer. Using Azurel's stable S1 channel as the foundation, Rakoron teaches Rizer to contain and command his fury.
6. **S2: Controlled Rage** — Rizer unlocks **S2**, a primal survival form that converts rage into focused power rather than destruction.
7. **The Hunt Resumes** — Rizer identifies the Seers' trail leading into Xilnar.

## VII — Xilnar, Spiritlands

**Gemlord:** Obsidius, the Onyxlord  
**Story function:** Confronting death-energy, meeting the only Gemlord still walking, and discovering the location/purpose of Rizer's captured parents.

### Main-mission draft

1. **Lanterns Going Dark** — Xilnar's Elder reports that souls are disappearing before reaching the Last Lantern.
2. **The Walking Lord's Trail** — Rizer follows messages left for Obsidius across the district.
3. **Blackwake Rising** — Seer rituals turn grief and unresolved memories into hostile Spirit phenomena.
4. **Xenoxil's Six** — Rizer battles six Seer grunts guarding the spiritual siphon. Xenoxil appears with Xilnar but does not yet battle Rizer directly.
5. **The Parents' Purpose** — Rizer learns that his mother is being used for biological/Astralite knowledge and his father for Zyrex bonding research. Together, their expertise could help the Seers dominate both mortals and Zyrex.
6. **The Onyxlord Walks** — Obsidius acknowledges Rizer's S2 discipline and joins the movement against the takeover.
7. **Road to Civilization** — The Seers move their captives and resources toward Baelgor, where control of government and public belief could legitimize their rule.

## VIII — Baelgor, Humanoidlands

**Gemlord:** Ambrevon, the Amberlord  
**Story function:** Return to Rizer's former home, destruction in Baelgor, the Pledge of Baelgor, and a political phase of the takeover.

### Main-mission draft

1. **Homecoming to Ruin** — Rizer returns to discover that his Baelgor home has been destroyed and the district is divided by fear.
2. **The University Under Watch** — Baelgor University becomes a center of resistance and competing propaganda.
3. **History of the First Bond** — At the Hall of First Settlement, Rizer learns how Novarius and Rakoron ended the Human–Zyrex War.
4. **The Pledge Rewritten** — Seer collaborators attempt to reinterpret the Pledge of Baelgor to justify emergency control over Zyrex and other districts.
5. **The Tenfold Crisis** — Rizer exposes the collaborators before representatives of the districts.
6. **Ambrevon's Standard** — Ambrevon tests whether Rizer can lead civilization without demanding obedience from everyone beneath him.
7. **The Captive Route** — Records reveal that the Seers transferred Rizer's parents through Thardun's automated infrastructure toward Korathen.

## IX — Thardun, Mechlands

**Gemlord:** Oathane, the Anomaly — missing  
**Story function:** Technology without divine oversight, search for Oathane, and disruption of the Seers' planetary control machine.

### Main-mission draft

1. **The Land Without Its God** — Thardun's Elder explains how the district evolved after Oathane vanished.
2. **Orders No One Gave** — The Orphan Foundry begins manufacturing components for the Seers without any visible command.
3. **System Defect: Rizer** — The Precision Ministry classifies Rizer's unpredictable behavior as a threat to planetary stability.
4. **The Anomaly Engine** — Rizer activates the device intended to locate Oathane and discovers that the Gemlord's disappearance is connected to Oatheus and Korathen.
5. **Break the Control Grid** — Rizer's faction destroys or liberates the machinery meant to synchronize all ten districts under Seer authority.
6. **Signal of the Missing** — A fragment of Oathane's presence helps open the route into Korathen but does not fully resolve the Gemlord's fate.
7. **March on the Ultralands** — The united districts prepare for the final assault.

## X — Korathen, Ultralands

**Gemlord:** Oatheus, the Ultralord — missing  
**Story function:** Council of Gemlords, Mothergem, rescue of Rizer's parents, the three-Seer battle, and prevention of the planetary takeover.

### Main-mission draft

1. **The Empty Authority** — Korathen's Elder reveals that the Seers intend to occupy the authority abandoned with Oatheus's disappearance.
2. **Tribunal Without a Lord** — Rizer secures recognition from representatives of the districts and proves the Seers cannot lawfully inherit Zyraxis.
3. **The Council at Mothergem** — The available Gemlords gather at the Mothergem Sanctum. Their ancient non-intervention pact prevents them from simply destroying the mortal Seers, but they can empower and support Rizer's mortal-led faction.
4. **Rescue at the Sanctum** — Rizer locates and rescues his parents before their combined research can complete the bonding and biological control system.
5. **Orryx and Ophira, Reclaimed** — The two Chiefs return with enhanced forms or restored anchors, forcing Rizer to prove that their earlier defeats were not accidents.
6. **The Flesh Lord's Claim** — Xenoxil attempts to merge mortal transformation, Zyrex bonding, spirit energy, technology, and district authority into a single takeover ritual.
7. **Battle for Zyraxis** — Rizer, his faction, and his allies battle all three Seer leaders at Korathen. S1 supplies control and perception; S2 supplies the disciplined fury necessary to overpower Xenoxil without losing himself.
8. **The Throne Remains Empty** — The takeover fails. Rizer refuses to replace one absolute ruler with another and restores authority to the districts while the mystery of Oathane and Oatheus remains open.
9. **Toward the Novarian Challenge** — Rizer's defense of Zyraxis qualifies or positions him for the Novarian Challenge, carrying the story into the next major phase.

# Thirty District Lore Buildings

## Malezor

1. **The Fanghall** — An ancient bone-and-timber council hall where the Elder judges territorial disputes and gives main missions.
2. **The Bloodscent Lodge** — A hunters' guild devoted to tracking, predator behavior, rescue, and Beast-type bonding.
3. **The First Den** — Malezor's oldest sanctuary for abandoned young Zyrex deciding whether to bond or return to the wild.

## Zarvane

4. **The Resonance Spire** — A crystalline tower monitoring the district's emotional, Aura, and Astralite frequencies.
5. **The Quiet Between** — A temple that blocks outside frequencies and forces visitors to navigate through Aura perception.
6. **The Vibration Conservatory** — A musical laboratory that converts Aura patterns into sound to diagnose disturbances and damaged bonds.

## Andrannor

7. **CLUB VX** — A mutation-themed nightclub whose environment adapts to the species and creatures inside.
8. **The Morphic Menagerie** — A living research habitat whose controlled biomes reshape around rapidly evolving Zyrex.
9. **The Chimera Exchange** — A market for evolutionary materials with an illegal creature-trafficking operation concealed beneath it.

## Veridan

10. **The Root Parliament** — A living governing chamber grown inside the roots of a colossal ancient tree.
11. **The Seedvault** — A protected archive of seeds, spores, water, and genetic material from endangered ecosystems.
12. **The Overgrowth Hospice** — A symbiotic healing center whose plants can absorb too much from their patients.

## Netharion

13. **The Impossible Archive** — A forbidden library larger within than without, containing erased and future histories.
14. **The Null Observatory** — A geometric facility that studies reality fractures and impossible signals.
15. **The Crooked House** — A shifting residence whose doorways continually rearrange its trapped interior.

## Vorashil

16. **The Shape Embassy** — A diplomatic structure translating thought through light, pressure, scent, mathematics, and geometry.
17. **The Unmouth Academy** — A school for communication with collective minds and beings without speech.
18. **The Manybody Habitat** — One interconnected building operated by a single consciousness living through hundreds of bodies.

## Xilnar

19. **The Last Lantern** — A sanctuary where wandering souls are stabilized before crossing or becoming hostile.
20. **The Blackwake Chapel** — A funeral temple that prevents grief and memory from accumulating into Spirit threats.
21. **The Walking Lord's Station** — A hidden wayhouse where reports and offerings are left for the traveling Obsidius.

## Baelgor

22. **Baelgor University** — A diverse party university that also preserves humanoid history and records of Novarius.
23. **The Hall of First Settlement** — A civic museum built above the first humanoid settlement on Zyraxis.
24. **The Tenfold Forum** — A court and legislative chamber where all ten districts debate law, trade, migration, and the Pledge.

## Thardun

25. **The Orphan Foundry** — A self-operating factory that invents machines without understanding why.
26. **The Precision Ministry** — An algorithmic government tower that increasingly treats unpredictability as a defect.
27. **The Anomaly Engine** — A sealed machine built around Oathane's last known energy signature.

## Korathen

28. **The Empty Throne** — Oatheus's abandoned seat, which violently rejects unworthy claimants.
29. **The Tribunal of Ten** — A sacred fortress once used to settle disputes involving districts, Gemlords, and the Pledge.
30. **The Mothergem Sanctum** — Zyraxis's holiest structure and the gathering place of the Council of Gemlords.

## Draft Status

This document is a narrative progression draft rather than final mission canon. Building names and concepts are retained as the current approved roster, while individual missions can be kept, rejected, renamed, reordered, or expanded district by district.
